## What is scoliosis?

Scoliosis is a sideways curve of the spine. It can be identified during childhood or adolescence, or it may develop or become more noticeable later in life. The curve pattern and symptoms vary from person to person.

## Possible signs and symptoms

Signs can include uneven shoulders or hips, a prominent shoulder blade, a visible curve when bending forward, back fatigue or discomfort. Some people have no noticeable symptoms.

## How is scoliosis evaluated?

A clinician may review health history, observe posture and movement, perform an examination and recommend imaging when appropriate. Evaluation considers age, stage of growth, curve characteristics and symptoms.

## Treatment options

Treatment can range from observation and monitoring to rehabilitation, bracing or specialist referral. The best approach depends on the type and severity of the curve and the individual's needs.

## When should you seek care?

Schedule an evaluation for newly noticed spinal curvature, persistent pain, changes in balance or walking, numbness, weakness or a rapid change in posture.
