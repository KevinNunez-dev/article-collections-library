## What sciatica is

Sciatica is leg and low-back pain that follows the path of the sciatic nerve, often through the buttock and down the back of the leg. It can feel like burning, aching, tingling, or sharp pain. Causes vary. Disc problems, spinal narrowing, and joint irritation can be involved, and so can tight muscles in the hip and glutes. See [piriformis syndrome vs sciatica](/health/piriformis-syndrome-vs-sciatica/) and [how to tell sciatica from piriformis pain](/health/how-to-tell-sciatica-from-piriformis-pain/).

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot, or a dropping foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe unrelenting pain
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma
