## Scoliosis and Physical Therapy: Manage Your Spinal Health

Scoliosis involves abnormal spinal curvature affecting posture, function, and sometimes pain. While scoliosis can't be eliminated through therapy, physical therapy plays an important role in managing symptoms, maintaining posture, and preventing progression.

At **RPT Clinic**, we provide personalized programs supporting scoliosis management throughout life.

## Understanding Scoliosis

Scoliosis is characterized by:
- Abnormal spinal curves (sideways curvature)
- Potential rotation of vertebrae
- Often developing in youth
- Can progress over time without management
- Affects posture and sometimes function
- May cause pain or limitations

## Common Scoliosis-Related Challenges

- Postural asymmetry and appearance concerns
- Back pain or localized discomfort
- Reduced spinal mobility
- Muscle imbalance
- Breathing limitations (severe curves)
- Functional limitations
- Progression of curvature

## Physical Therapy for Scoliosis

Therapy goals typically include:
- Managing pain and discomfort
- Improving posture and alignment
- Improving spinal mobility
- Addressing muscle imbalances
- Preventing progression
- Maintaining functional abilities
- Building confidence

## Scoliosis Through the Lifespan

**Childhood Onset**: Early monitoring and therapy can prevent progression

**Adolescent Progression**: During growth years, scoliosis can progress quickly; consistent therapy is important

**Adulthood**: Stable curves require maintenance therapy and activity

**Older Adults**: Degenerative changes may compound scoliosis; therapy helps manage symptoms

## Benefits of Scoliosis Therapy

- Improved posture and appearance
- Reduced pain and discomfort
- Reduced curve progression
- Maintained functional abilities
- Improved confidence and self-esteem
- Better breathing and endurance
