## Who treats tennis elbow near Detroit?

Tennis elbow usually describes pain on the outside of the elbow associated with the tendons that help extend the wrist. You do not have to play tennis to experience it. Work tools, lifting, household tasks, and sports can all involve repeated gripping or wrist movement.

A primary care clinician, sports medicine physician, orthopedic or hand specialist, or physical therapist may be involved. The right provider depends on your symptoms, history, and whether a medical evaluation is needed. See our [tennis elbow guide](/health/tennis-elbow) and [MedlinePlus's overview](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000449.htm).

## What should the evaluation include?

Describe where the pain is located and which tasks provoke it: lifting a mug, turning a handle, typing, or using a racket. Mention how much you grip at work, whether symptoms followed an injury, and any tingling or numbness.

An examination may include the elbow, wrist, grip, and relevant movements. A clinician may also consider the neck or nerve symptoms when appropriate. Ask what findings support tennis elbow and what else could explain the pain.

## When to seek medical care

Seek urgent medical assessment for an obviously deformed elbow after an injury or a hot swollen joint with fever. Sudden substantial weakness after injury, persistent numbness, or pain that continues to worsen should also be assessed by a clinician.

## Questions to ask about your plan

- What changes can I make to gripping, tools, or exercise while symptoms settle?
- Which exercises fit my current tolerance, and how should they progress?
- Would a brace or other option be useful in my circumstances?
- How will we measure improvement in the tasks that matter to me?
- When would testing or a specialist referral be appropriate?

Avoid forcing activities that cause severe or worsening pain. A gradual plan is more useful than an unsupported promise of a fixed recovery date. Compare [golfer's elbow](/health/golfers-elbow), which generally affects the inner elbow.
