## What is a herniated disc?

A herniated disc occurs when disc material extends through a weakened area of the outer disc layer. Depending on its location, it may irritate or compress nearby nerve tissue.

## Common symptoms of a herniated disc

- Neck or back pain.
- Pain that travels into an arm, buttock or leg.
- Tingling, numbness or burning sensations.
- Weakness or changes in coordination.

## What can contribute to it?

Disc changes can occur over time and may also follow injury or repeated loading. Symptoms are influenced by the location of the disc and the structures involved.

## Treatment options

A care plan can include activity guidance, rehabilitation, pain-management discussions, medicines, imaging or referral depending on the severity and pattern of symptoms.

## Emergency warning signs

Seek urgent care for loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the groin, rapidly worsening weakness, severe symptoms after trauma or sudden trouble walking.
