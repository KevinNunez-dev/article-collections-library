## What is a herniated disc?

A herniated disc occurs when disc material pushes through a weakened area of its outer layer. It can irritate or compress a nearby nerve. Some disc changes seen on imaging do not cause symptoms, so a clinician considers the examination and symptoms alongside any scan.

## Can a herniated disc cause pain or weakness?

Depending on its location, a herniated disc may cause back or neck pain, pain traveling into a leg or arm, tingling, numbness, or weakness. These symptoms can have other causes. New or worsening weakness needs prompt medical attention; see the emergency signs below.

## What treatment options should you discuss?

Care depends on the individual and may include advice about activity, physical therapy, medication, injections, or surgical assessment. Surgery is not needed for every person, but some symptoms require urgent specialist evaluation. Discuss benefits, risks, and timing with the clinician managing your condition rather than delaying recommended care.

## Can Robotic Precision Therapy repair or decompress a disc?

No. RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when an assessment suggests muscular contributors are appropriate to address. It does not repair or reposition a disc, decompress the spine, or release a compressed nerve. Results vary, and RPT does not replace necessary medical care.

See [spinal decompression versus RPT](/health/spinal-decompression-vs-robotic-precision-therapy/) and [sciatica](/health/sciatica/) for related questions.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek emergency care for new loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness in the groin or saddle area, or rapidly worsening weakness in a leg or arm. Seek urgent medical assessment for symptoms after major trauma or severe and worsening pain.
