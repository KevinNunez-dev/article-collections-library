## What does poor posture mean?

Posture describes how your body is positioned while sitting, standing or moving. Rather than searching for one perfect position, it can be more helpful to build movement variety, strength and a workspace that supports your tasks.

## Signs your setup may need adjustment

- Regular neck, shoulder or back discomfort during work.
- Symptoms that increase after long periods in one position.
- Difficulty seeing a screen without leaning forward.
- A desk, chair or device setup that does not fit your body or work.

## Practical posture and ergonomics tips

- Change positions regularly during the day.
- Keep frequently used items within easy reach.
- Adjust screen height and seating for your task.
- Build breaks for walking, stretching or changing position.
- Ask a clinician about persistent pain or movement limitations.

## When should you seek care?

Seek evaluation for persistent pain, symptoms after injury, numbness, weakness, severe headaches or other symptoms that affect daily function.
