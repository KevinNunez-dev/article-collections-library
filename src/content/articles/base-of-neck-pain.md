## Where Neck and Shoulder Pain Meet

The base of the neck is a busy transition area. Muscles from the head, shoulder blades, collarbone, and upper back all attach or pass nearby. Pain can be dull and heavy after a workday, sharp when turning the head, or paired with shoulder tightness and reduced range of motion.

## Common Contributors to Base-of-Neck Pain

Screen work, driving, looking down at a phone, stress, overhead work, and carrying heavy bags can all increase demand on the upper trapezius, levator scapulae, and deep neck muscles. When the upper back becomes stiff and the shoulder blades lose coordinated movement, the neck may compensate.

## Assessment and Targeted Pressure at RPT Clinic

RPT Clinic evaluates the full neck-shoulder-upper-back pattern before recommending care. Robotic Precision Therapy can provide consistent, gentle pressure to selected muscular areas as part of a plan designed around the individual's symptoms and movement findings. The approach may be combined with mobility, activity, and ergonomic recommendations.

## Important Warning Signs

Get urgent medical care for neck pain following a high-impact injury; new severe headache; fever with neck stiffness; trouble speaking, walking, or seeing; severe dizziness; or arm weakness that is worsening. Persistent tingling or pain traveling down the arm should be assessed.
