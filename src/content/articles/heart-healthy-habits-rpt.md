## Heart-Healthy Habits: Exercise for Cardiovascular Wellness

Cardiovascular health is built through consistent movement and exercise. Regular physical activity reduces heart disease risk, improves circulation, and supports overall health.

**However, many people struggle with consistent exercise due to pain, muscle imbalance, or limited mobility.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we help patients achieve pain-free movement enabling consistent exercise routines that support heart health. Our approach combines therapy with guidance on heart-healthy habits.

## Why Movement Matters for Heart Health

Regular exercise:
- Strengthens heart muscle
- Improves circulation
- Reduces blood pressure
- Improves cholesterol profile
- Supports healthy weight
- Reduces heart disease risk
- Improves longevity

## Recommended Exercise for Heart Health

**Aerobic Exercise**: 150 minutes weekly of moderate-intensity activity
- Walking, jogging, cycling, swimming, dancing
- Accumulate in sessions of at least 10 minutes

**Strength Training**: 2+ days weekly
- Builds lean muscle supporting metabolism
- Protects bone density

**Flexibility**: Regular stretching and mobility work
- Maintains range of motion
- Supports exercise capacity

**Consistency**: Most important factor for long-term benefit

## Common Barriers to Heart-Healthy Exercise

- Chronic pain limiting movement
- Muscle tension reducing exercise capacity
- Poor posture affecting breathing and endurance
- Fear of re-injury
- Limited mobility
- Fatigue from poor muscle mechanics

## How We Support Heart-Healthy Habits

### Address Physical Barriers

Better mechanics enable efficient, sustainable exercise

## Heart-Healthy Habit Building

**Start Gradually**: Begin with achievable movement goals

**Build Consistency**: Regular movement matters more than intensity

**Make It Enjoyable**: Choose activities you enjoy for long-term adherence

**Track Progress**: Monitor improvements encouraging continued effort

**Stay Social**: Group activities improve adherence

## Benefits of Heart-Healthy Habits

- Reduced cardiovascular disease risk
- Improved heart function
- Better circulation and blood pressure
- Improved cholesterol profile
- Healthy weight maintenance
- Improved energy and mood
- Better longevity and quality of life
