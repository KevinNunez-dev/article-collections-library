## Groin Pain in Men

**Groin pain in men** is frequently an adductor (inner thigh) strain or a hip-related problem, particularly in people who sprint, kick, or change direction. It can also come from the abdominal wall, nerves, or conditions that need medical care. For the full overview, see [groin pain](/health/groin-pain).

## Safety First

<div class="urgent-box">
<p class="cta-title">Seek urgent medical care for groin pain with:</p>
<ul>
<li>Sudden testicular pain or swelling</li>
<li>A new, firm, or painful groin bulge</li>
<li>Severe abdominal pain, vomiting, or fever</li>
<li>Blood in the urine or pain with urination</li>
<li>Inability to bear weight or pain after major trauma</li>
</ul>

## Common Musculoskeletal Causes

- **Adductor strain:** pain on the inner thigh or near the pubic bone with sprinting, kicking, or wide-stance movements. See [muscle strain](/health/muscle-strain).
- **Hip joint contributors:** pain with deep flexion or rotation. See [hip pain](/health/hip-pain).
- **Abdominal wall and core overload:** pain with sit-ups, coughing, or twisting.
- **Low back and nerve referral:** see [lower back pain](/health/lower-back-pain).

## Context Clues

| Context | What it may suggest |
| --- | --- |
| Sprinting or cutting | Adductor strain, hip contributor |
| Kicking | Adductor or hip flexor |
| Coughing or lifting | Abdominal wall; hernia needs medical exam |
| Hip rotation or deep squat | Hip joint |

## How Physical Therapy Helps

A therapist reviews hip and spine mobility, adductor and core strength, gait, and sport demands, then builds a gradual return-to-activity plan.
