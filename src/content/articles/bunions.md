## What is a bunion?

A bunion is a bony bump at the joint where the big toe meets the foot. The big toe may gradually angle toward the other toes, changing the shape and mechanics of the joint.

## Common bunion symptoms

- A visible bump at the base of the big toe.
- Pain, redness or swelling around the joint.
- Shoe pressure or difficulty finding comfortable footwear.
- Calluses or changes in walking.

## What can contribute to bunions?

Foot structure, joint mechanics, family history, footwear pressure and some health conditions may play a role. An evaluation can help distinguish bunion pain from other sources of forefoot pain.

## Treatment options

Care can include footwear changes, padding, activity modification, pain-management options, exercises or referral to a foot specialist. Recommendations depend on symptoms and function.

## When should you seek care?

Schedule an evaluation for persistent foot pain, skin irritation, difficulty walking, sudden swelling or symptoms that affect work, exercise or daily activities.
