## Elbow Pain: Look at the Whole Arm

The elbow links the upper arm with the forearm and is involved in lifting, gripping, throwing, and pushing. Pain may be on the inner or outer elbow, at the tip, or deep in the joint. Its timing and trigger are important parts of an assessment.

## Common Contributors

Repetitive gripping, racquet sports, lifting, overhead activity, and prolonged work with tools can increase elbow load. Forearm muscle tension and altered shoulder mechanics may also influence how force moves through the elbow.

## RPT Care for Muscular Patterns

RPT Clinic evaluates the elbow alongside the wrist, forearm, shoulder, and neck. When appropriate, Robotic Precision Therapy may provide consistent, gentle pressure to selected soft tissues to support movement and reduce muscular guarding.

## When to Seek Care

Seek urgent assessment for a deformed elbow, severe swelling, inability to move the arm, fever with a hot joint, or numbness after an injury. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency elbow pain.
