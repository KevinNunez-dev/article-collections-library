## What sciatica feels like

People describe burning, tingling, aching, or sharp pain that starts in the low back or buttock and travels down a leg. It can feel worse when sitting, bending, or standing for long periods. ## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe unrelenting pain
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma
