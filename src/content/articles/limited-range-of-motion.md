## What Is Limited Range of Motion?

Range of motion describes how far a body part can move in a direction, such as turning the neck, reaching overhead, bending the knee, or flexing the ankle. Reduced range can be painful, stiff, weak, or simply feel blocked.

## Common Contributors

Pain-related guarding, swelling, injury, joint conditions, muscle tension, and inactivity can reduce comfortable movement. The source may be at the painful joint or in a related area that affects the movement pattern.

## Assessment and Targeted Care

RPT Clinic evaluates movement quality, symptoms, and nearby joints before recommending care. When muscular tension is a contributor, Robotic Precision Therapy may use gentle, precise pressure to selected soft tissues within a broader plan for comfortable movement.

## When to Seek Care

Seek prompt evaluation for sudden loss of movement after injury, a hot swollen joint, deformity, severe pain, or weakness and numbness. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency mobility concerns.
