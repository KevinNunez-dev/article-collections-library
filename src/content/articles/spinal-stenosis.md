## What is spinal stenosis?

Spinal stenosis means narrowing of one or more spaces in the spine. When narrowing affects nerve tissue, it may contribute to pain, numbness, weakness or changes in walking tolerance.

## Common symptoms

- Back or neck discomfort.
- Pain, tingling or numbness in an arm or leg.
- Leg heaviness or weakness when standing or walking.
- Symptoms that improve with rest or a change in position.

## How is spinal stenosis evaluated?

A clinician considers symptoms, medical history, movement and neurological findings. Imaging may be used when it is needed to clarify the source of symptoms or guide care.

## Treatment approaches

Options may include activity modification, rehabilitation, medication discussions, injections or referral. The right plan depends on symptoms and how they affect daily function.

## When should you get urgent help?

Seek urgent evaluation for new bladder or bowel changes, numbness around the groin, rapidly worsening weakness, major trauma or sudden inability to walk safely.
