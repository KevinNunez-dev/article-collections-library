## Forearm Pain and Repetitive Loading

The forearm contains muscles that bend and straighten the wrist and fingers, rotate the palm, and support grip. Pain may develop gradually with computer work, tools, racquet sports, or lifting, or it can follow a strain.

## Movement Factors

A sudden increase in activity, repeated wrist positions, limited shoulder movement, and fatigue can change how forearm tissues are loaded. Symptoms may also accompany wrist or elbow discomfort.

## Targeted Care

RPT Clinic assesses the full upper-limb pattern. Where appropriate, Robotic Precision Therapy can apply gentle, controlled pressure to selected muscles that contribute to tension or restricted movement. Treatment is part of an individualized care plan.

## When to Seek Assessment

Seek prompt care for visible swelling, bruising after injury, weakness, pain with a hot or red area, or persistent numbness. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency forearm symptoms.
