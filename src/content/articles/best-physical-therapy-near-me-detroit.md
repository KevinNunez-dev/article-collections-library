## The Problem With Manual-Only Physical Therapy

Traditional physical therapy is valuable, but it has a hard physical limit: **the therapist's own body**. A human hand fatigues. Grip strength drops after 60-90 seconds of firm, sustained pressure. Therapists must alternate techniques, take breaks, and pace themselves across a full patient schedule.

But many root muscles—the psoas, deep glutes, suboccipitals, and quadratus lumborum among them—require **consistent, targeted pressure held far longer than a fatiguing hand can deliver** to fully release their protective tension.

This is why so many Detroit-area patients cycle through PT, feel temporary relief, and then watch the pain return within weeks.
