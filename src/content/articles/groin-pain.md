## Groin Pain Needs a Thoughtful Assessment

The groin is where the inner thigh meets the pelvis. Pain may develop after sprinting, cutting, kicking, lifting, or a sudden change of direction. It can also be felt with hip movement or alongside abdominal symptoms, so location alone cannot determine the cause.

## Muscular Contributors

The adductor muscles on the inner thigh help control leg movement. Rapid sport demands, reduced hip mobility, and uneven loading can contribute to muscular strain or guarding. Hip and low-back movement may also influence symptoms.

## RPT Clinic Care

When assessment suggests a musculoskeletal pattern, RPT Clinic may use gentle, targeted pressure to selected tissues around the hip and thigh as part of a gradual return-to-activity plan. Care is individualized and includes referral when symptoms suggest another cause.

## Seek Urgent Care For

Get urgent help for severe abdominal pain, vomiting, fever, a new groin bulge, sudden testicular pain, inability to bear weight, or pain after major trauma. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency groin pain.
