## Multiple Sclerosis and Therapy: Maintain Your Independence

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a neurological condition affecting movement, strength, balance, and fatigue levels. Physical therapy plays an important role in maintaining functional abilities and independence.

At **RPT Clinic**, we provide personalized physical therapy programs supporting MS management. Our approach addresses MS-specific challenges while respecting individual needs and energy levels.

## Understanding Multiple Sclerosis

MS affects:
- Muscle strength and endurance
- Movement control and coordination
- Balance and proprioception
- Spasticity (muscle tightness)
- Fatigue levels
- Functional mobility

## Common MS-Related Challenges

- Weakness affecting mobility
- Spasticity limiting movement
- Balance deficits increasing fall risk
- Fatigue limiting activity
- Difficulty with stairs and walking
- Loss of fine motor skills
- Difficulty with daily living activities

## Physical Therapy for MS

Therapy addresses:
- Maintaining walking ability
- Managing spasticity
- Improving balance
- Maintaining strength
- Supporting mobility
- Improving endurance
- Building confidence

### Individualized Assessment
We evaluate your specific MS challenges, functional limitations, spasticity patterns, and personal goals.

### Personalized Program Design
Programs are tailored to your specific needs, energy levels, and rehabilitation potential.

### Strength and Endurance Training
Maintaining strength and endurance despite MS challenges.

### Balance and Fall Prevention
Improving balance reducing fall risk.

### Spasticity Management
Techniques and exercises reducing spasticity and improving comfort.

### Energy Management
Pacing and programming respecting MS fatigue realities.

## Benefits of MS Therapy

- Maintained walking ability
- Reduced spasticity
- Improved balance and confidence
- Maintained strength
- Reduced fall risk
- Improved functional independence
- Better quality of life

## Therapy Frequency and Duration

MS therapy typically involves:
- 2-3 sessions weekly initially
- Ongoing maintenance therapy
- Home exercise programs for between-session activity
- Regular reassessment and program adjustment
