## Questions to ask any provider

Searches around Rochester Hills turn up several kinds of practices. Before you book, ask:

1. What will my assessment include?
2. How long are visits, and how often will I be seen?
3. What goals and timelines are reasonable for my findings?
4. How will we measure progress?
5. What does it cost, and is insurance accepted?
6. When would you refer me to a physician or spine specialist?

## When sciatica has other causes

Sciatica can be linked to a [herniated disc](/health/herniated-disc/), [spinal stenosis](/health/spinal-stenosis/), or irritation of the joints near the pelvis. Tight muscles in the hip and glutes can also contribute. See [glute pain](/health/glute-pain/) and [how to loosen tight hips](/health/how-to-loosen-tight-hips/) for the muscular side.

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe unrelenting pain
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma
