## What is fibromyalgia?

Fibromyalgia is a chronic condition associated with widespread pain and increased sensitivity to pain. It may also affect sleep, energy, mood, concentration and everyday function.

## Common fibromyalgia symptoms

- Widespread aching, burning or tenderness.
- Fatigue that does not improve with rest.
- Sleep that is unrefreshing.
- Stiffness, headaches or digestive symptoms.
- Trouble concentrating, sometimes called brain fog.

## How is fibromyalgia diagnosed?

There is no single test that confirms fibromyalgia. A clinician reviews your symptom pattern, health history and examination findings and may order tests to evaluate other possible causes.

## What can a care plan include?

Care often combines education, movement that fits your tolerance, sleep support, stress management, mental-health support and medication discussions when appropriate.

## When should you speak with a clinician?

Make an appointment for persistent widespread pain, unexplained fatigue, sleep changes or symptoms that interfere with work, relationships or daily life.
