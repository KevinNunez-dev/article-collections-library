## What Is Gluteal Pain?

**Gluteal pain**, often called **glute pain** or buttock pain, is discomfort in the muscles at the back of the hip. It may be a deep ache, a sharp pain with stairs, burning at the sit bone, or soreness when lying on one side. The area includes the gluteus maximus, medius, and minimus, deeper hip rotators such as the piriformis, and tendons attaching around the hip.

## Common Causes

- **Glute muscle strain or overuse:** new training, hills, or sudden increases.
- **Tendon irritation near the hip:** pain on the outer hip and glute, worse lying on the side or climbing stairs.
- **Sit bone and hamstring attachment irritation:** pain with sitting.
- **Piriformis tightness:** deep buttock pain. See [piriformis syndrome vs sciatica](/health/piriformis-syndrome-vs-sciatica).
- **Sacroiliac joint irritation:** pain near the dimple of the low back. See [sacroiliac joint pain](/health/sacroiliac-joint-pain).
- **Low back referral:** nerve or disc irritation can refer to the buttock. See [lower back pain](/health/lower-back-pain) and [sciatica](/health/sciatica).
- **Hip contributors:** see [hip pain](/health/hip-pain).

## Pain by Location and Trigger

| Where or when | Possible contributors |
| --- | --- |
| Upper outer glute, side lying | Tendon irritation, glute medius overload |
| Deep in the buttock | Piriformis, deep rotators, nerve irritation |
| Sit bone, when sitting | Hamstring attachment, sit bone sensitivity |
| Near the low back dimple | Sacroiliac joint |
| Stairs or hills | Glute weakness or overuse |

## Safe Relief for Mild Pain

1. Break up sitting and change positions often.
2. Use heat or cold, whichever eases symptoms.
3. Walk in short, comfortable bouts.
4. Use a pillow between the knees when side lying.
5. Reintroduce gentle strengthening such as bridges as comfort allows.

## How Physical Therapy Helps

A therapist checks hip and back mobility, glute and trunk strength, gait, and sitting and training habits. RPT Clinic can use gentle, targeted Robotic Precision Therapy pressure on selected glute and hip muscles when assessment suggests a muscular contributor, combined with graded strengthening and activity guidance.
