## Golf Performance: Improve Your Game

Golf performance depends on a coordinated kinetic chain: proper core stability, hip rotation, shoulder mobility, and muscle balance all contribute to power, consistency, and accuracy.

**Many golfers with muscle imbalances experience compromised swing mechanics, reduced distance, inconsistent shots, and golf-related injuries.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we use **Robotic Precision Therapy** combined with performance training to enhance swing mechanics, increase power, and prevent golf injuries. Most golfers notice improved performance within 4-6 sessions.

## Understanding Golf Biomechanics

Golf swing requires:
- Hip mobility and rotation
- Core stability and power
- Shoulder mobility and stability
- Proper sequencing of muscle activation
- Coordinated movement patterns

Muscle imbalances that compromise golf performance include:
- Tight hip flexors limiting hip rotation
- Weak glutes failing to stabilize pelvis
- Tight chest limiting shoulder turn
- Weak core failing to generate power
- Poor shoulder mobility limiting backswing

## Common Golf Performance Issues

- Reduced driving distance
- Inconsistent ball striking
- Loss of power in swing
- Reduced accuracy
- Difficulty with certain shots
- Pain limiting full swing
- Poor rotation limiting mechanics

## The Performance Challenge: Muscle Imbalance + Poor Movement Sequencing

Golf performance suffers from:

**Tight Hip Flexors** limiting hip rotation necessary for power

**Weak Glutes** failing to stabilize pelvis during swing

**Tight Chest** limiting shoulder turn and backswing

**Weak Core** failing to generate rotational power

**Poor Shoulder Mobility** limiting swing range of motion

**Compensatory Patterns** creating inconsistency and reduced power
