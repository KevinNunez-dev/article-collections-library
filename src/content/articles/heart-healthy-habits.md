## Start with your care plan

Heart health is personal. Your age, health history, medications, family history and current activity level all matter. A preventive visit is a useful place to ask questions and review your individual goals.

## Everyday topics to consider

- Choosing enjoyable movement you can do consistently.
- Building meals around foods that fit your needs and preferences.
- Making time for sleep and stress management.
- Avoiding tobacco and discussing alcohol use with your clinician.
