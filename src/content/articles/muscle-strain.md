## What Is a Muscle Strain?

A muscle strain, often called a pulled muscle, occurs when muscle fibers are stretched beyond their tolerance or overloaded. Strains are common during sprinting, lifting, reaching, or a sudden movement, but can also develop gradually with repetitive use.

## Symptoms and Recovery

Symptoms may include pain, tenderness, stiffness, weakness, and sometimes bruising or swelling. The best next steps depend on severity, location, and function. A gradual return to comfortable movement is often more useful than pushing through sharp pain.

## RPT Clinic's Role

After acute injury concerns are addressed, RPT Clinic can assess muscular guarding, movement restriction, and compensations that may remain during recovery. Robotic Precision Therapy may use gentle targeted pressure when appropriate within a broader recovery plan.

## Seek Prompt Evaluation For

A sudden pop, large bruise, visible deformity, inability to use the limb, or rapidly worsening pain needs medical evaluation. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency muscular recovery support.
