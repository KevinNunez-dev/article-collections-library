## Think Movement, Not Perfection

Posture is the ability to use different positions comfortably. Holding any position for too long can create fatigue. Build regular movement breaks into work, driving, and leisure time instead of trying to stay rigidly upright.

## Make Your Setup Easier

Adjust screens, keyboards, chairs, and frequently used items to limit sustained reaching and looking down. Small changes that fit your daily routine are more likely to last than a major overhaul.

## Address Persistent Tension

RPT Clinic can evaluate whether muscular tension and movement restriction contribute to neck, shoulder, or back symptoms. Gentle targeted pressure may be part of care when appropriate. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent posture-related discomfort.
