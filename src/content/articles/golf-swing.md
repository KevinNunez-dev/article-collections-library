## Why can golf cause pain?

Golf includes repeated rotation, bending, gripping and weight transfer. These movements can place demand on the back, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and knees, especially when activity changes quickly or recovery is limited.

## Common areas of golf-related pain

- Low back and mid-back.
- Shoulder and neck.
- Inner or outer elbow.
- Wrist and hand.
- Hip, knee or ankle.

## Five ways to reduce injury risk

- Warm up with movements that prepare you to rotate and walk.
- Increase practice and play volume gradually.
- Build general strength and conditioning.
- Address pain that changes your swing or daily function.
- Review equipment and technique with qualified professionals.

## When should you seek care?

Make an appointment for recurring pain, loss of motion, numbness, weakness, pain after a fall or symptoms that keep you from normal activity.
