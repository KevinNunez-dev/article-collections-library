## Who treats plantar fasciitis near Detroit?

A podiatrist, primary care clinician, sports medicine physician, orthopedic foot and ankle specialist, or physical therapist may help evaluate heel pain. The best starting point depends on your symptoms, health history, and whether medical tests or foot care are needed.

Plantar fasciitis often causes pain near the bottom of the heel with the first steps after rest. That pattern is useful information, but it is not proof: nerve irritation, injury, and other conditions can also cause foot pain. Read our [plantar fasciitis guide](/health/plantar-fasciitis) and [MedlinePlus's overview](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007021.htm).

## What should a heel pain evaluation cover?

Tell the provider when the pain started, where it is most intense, and what happens during the first steps in the morning, after sitting, and after prolonged standing. Mention a recent change in work, walking, running, or footwear. Report numbness, swelling, an injury, and any relevant medical conditions.

The examination may include the foot, ankle and calf movement, walking, and footwear or activity demands. Ask what supports the diagnosis, which other causes were considered, and whether imaging or a podiatry visit is appropriate.

## When to seek medical care

Seek urgent medical assessment for a hot swollen foot with fever or inability to bear weight after an injury. If you have diabetes and a foot wound, seek prompt medical advice. Persistent numbness, a wound that is not healing, or pain that continues to worsen also deserves medical assessment.

## Questions to ask about treatment

- What activity or footwear changes are reasonable for my daily routine?
- Which exercises are appropriate, and how should I adjust if pain increases?
- How will we measure progress in walking or standing?
- When should I see a podiatrist or consider another evaluation?
- What are the expected costs and follow-up arrangements?

Some people use a combination of activity changes, supportive footwear, and rehabilitation. There is no guaranteed timeline or single treatment that suits everyone. See also [heel pain](/health/heel-pain) and [foot pain](/health/foot-pain).
