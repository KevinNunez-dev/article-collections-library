## The short distinction

Physical therapy is a broad health profession and includes evaluation, education, exercise, functional training, and other interventions chosen for the person. “Robotic Precision Therapy” (RPT) is RPT Clinic’s name for an approach that uses the RX2600 during soft-tissue treatment. It is not robotic spine surgery, and it should not be treated as a proven replacement for all physical therapy.

The [RX2600 manufacturer site](https://rx2600.com/) and [RPT Clinic back-pain page](https://rptclinic.com/backpain/) are first-party descriptions. They can explain how a service is presented, but they are not independent head-to-head studies. The ACP guideline and the JOSPT 2021 physical therapy guideline discuss categories of care for low back pain; they do not prove the comparative value of every proprietary device. Review the [ACP guideline summary](https://www.acponline.org/acp-newsroom/american-college-of-physicians-issues-guideline-for-treating-nonradicular-low-back-pain) and [JOSPT guideline](https://www.jospt.org/doi/10.2519/jospt.2021.0508).

## Questions to ask before choosing

1. Who conducts the evaluation, and what does it cover?
2. What other options—including exercise-focused care—are reasonable?
3. What outcome or daily function will we track?
4. How soon will we reassess, and what would change the plan?
5. What are the visit duration, total quoted price, and likely out-of-pocket costs?
6. What warning signs prompt referral to a physician or surgeon?

If the answer is a guaranteed result, a universal “correct” number of visits, or a claim that a soft-tissue device repairs the spine, ask for independent evidence and a clearer explanation. A device can be an adjunct without being the only treatment or a treatment winner.

## Cost and logistics are part of an informed comparison

The approved pricing brief for this draft gives an initial assessment plus treatment as $225 for 1.5 hours and follow-ups at $150 per treatment hour, while the intake materials reportedly describe follow-ups as 50–60 minutes. These details were not independently retrievable from the clinic’s live pages on October 9, 2026. Ask the clinic to confirm current rates, duration, package inclusions, and payment terms in writing. A package should not be assumed to include the separate initial assessment.

Insurance may involve a deductible, copay, coinsurance, visit limit, authorization, or network restriction. The [HealthCare.gov total-cost guide](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/your-total-costs/) explains why premiums alone do not show total cost. Its glossary also defines [copayment](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/co-payment/), [coinsurance](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/co-insurance/), and [out-of-pocket maximum](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/out-of-pocket-maximum-limit/). Check your actual plan. Compare travel and unpaid work separately from the care price.
