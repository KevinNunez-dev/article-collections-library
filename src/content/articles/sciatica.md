## What is sciatica and what does it feel like?

Sciatica describes pain that travels along the path of the sciatic nerve, often from the lower back or buttock into one leg. It may feel sharp, burning, or electric and can occur with tingling, numbness, or weakness. Pain down the leg is not always sciatica; a clinician can help assess the cause.

## What can cause sciatic pain?

A herniated disc or narrowing around spinal nerves can irritate a nerve root. Other back, hip, or muscle problems can produce similar symptoms. A symptom pattern alone cannot establish which structure is responsible. See [herniated disc](/health/herniated-disc/) and [lower back pain](/health/understanding-lower-back-pain/) for context.

## How might Robotic Precision Therapy fit?

After an assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. This is not a diagnosis or a way to repair a disc or release a compressed nerve. It should not delay medical evaluation when symptoms suggest a nerve problem. Results vary.

Tell the clinician when the symptoms started, where they travel, whether you notice weakness, and which activities affect them. Ask what will be assessed, what other care may be appropriate, and how progress will be reviewed. Compare [spinal decompression and RPT](/health/spinal-decompression-vs-robotic-precision-therapy/).

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek emergency care for new bladder or bowel control changes, numbness in the groin or saddle area, or rapidly worsening leg weakness. Seek urgent medical assessment after major trauma or for severe or worsening symptoms. Persistent or changing leg pain also deserves a clinician's assessment.
