## What is sciatica?

Sciatica describes symptoms that follow the sciatic nerve pathway, often beginning in the low back or buttock and traveling into the leg. Symptoms can include pain, tingling, numbness or weakness.

## What does sciatica feel like?

People may describe sharp, burning, electric or shooting pain. Symptoms may worsen with certain positions, coughing, sneezing or prolonged sitting, but experiences vary.

## What can cause it?

Sciatic symptoms can occur when a nerve root in the lower spine is irritated or compressed. Disc changes, spinal narrowing, injury and other conditions can contribute. A clinician can evaluate the pattern and identify the most appropriate next step.

## How is sciatica treated?

Treatment may include activity guidance, therapeutic exercise, rehabilitation, pain-management options and further testing or referral when appropriate. Care should be based on your examination and health history.

## Red-flag symptoms

Seek urgent evaluation for rapidly progressing weakness, inability to control bladder or bowel function, numbness around the groin or symptoms following a serious injury.
