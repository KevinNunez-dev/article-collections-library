## What Is a Pain Clinic?

When people search for a **pain clinic near me** or **pain management doctors near me**, they may be looking for very different services. Physician-led pain practices typically offer medical evaluation, medications, and procedures such as injections. Physical therapy-based clinics focus on how you move, how your muscles are working, and how to build capacity over time.

| Approach | Typical focus |
| --- | --- |
| Physician-led pain management | Medical evaluation, medications, injections, procedural options |
| Physical therapy-based pain care | Movement assessment, soft-tissue work, strengthening, activity pacing |
| Combined care | Medical oversight alongside physical therapy |

## What to Expect at Your First Visit

1. **History:** where, when, and what changes your pain.
2. **Assessment:** movement, strength, posture, and muscle tension.
3. **Safety screening:** referral if a medical cause is suspected.
4. **Plan:** goals, treatment, and what you can do at home.
