## Wrist Pain and Daily Use

The wrist coordinates the hand with the forearm for typing, gripping, lifting, and sport. Symptoms may start after an injury or build gradually with repeated loading. Pain can occur on the thumb side, little-finger side, top, or palm side of the wrist.

## What Can Contribute?

Repetitive gripping, new strength training, long computer sessions, vibration tools, and a fall can all be relevant. Forearm muscles and tendons cross the wrist, while shoulder and neck movement can influence how the entire arm works.

## Assessment-Led RPT Care

RPT Clinic evaluates movement and symptom triggers before recommending care. For suitable muscular patterns, Robotic Precision Therapy may provide gentle, targeted pressure to selected forearm and upper-limb soft tissues as part of an individualized plan.

## When to Seek Care

Seek urgent assessment for deformity, severe swelling, inability to use the hand, numbness after an injury, or pain following a fall. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency wrist symptoms.
