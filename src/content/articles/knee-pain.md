## What is knee pain?

Knee pain is discomfort in or around the knee joint. It may develop suddenly after an injury or gradually with activity. The knee supports walking, stairs, squatting and many sports, so pain can affect everyday routines.

## Common symptoms to note

- Pain with walking, bending, stairs or exercise.
- Swelling, stiffness or warmth.
- Clicking, catching, locking or a feeling that the knee may give out.
- Reduced ability to straighten or bend the knee.

## Possible contributors

Knee symptoms can relate to activity changes, overuse, injury to structures around the joint, tendon or muscle concerns, arthritis and other medical conditions. Proper evaluation is important because symptoms can overlap.

## How is knee pain treated?

Treatment depends on the cause and severity. A clinician may recommend activity changes, rehabilitation, targeted exercise, pain-management options, imaging or referral when appropriate.

## When is urgent evaluation needed?

Seek prompt care after a serious injury, for obvious deformity, inability to bear weight, rapidly increasing swelling, fever with a hot swollen knee or new numbness or weakness.
