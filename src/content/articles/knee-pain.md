## Why might your knee hurt?

Knee pain can follow a twist or fall, or appear gradually with walking, stairs, squatting, or sport. Possible causes include tendon irritation, arthritis, ligament or cartilage injury, and pain around the kneecap. Muscles around the knee and hip may also contribute, but symptoms do not prove a single cause.

## What details help with assessment?

Describe where the pain is, any injury, swelling, locking or giving way, and whether you can bear weight. Note what activities make it worse and whether symptoms are changing. A clinician can determine whether further medical assessment is needed and discuss options such as activity changes, rehabilitation exercises, and other care.

## Can Robotic Precision Therapy be part of knee pain care?

After assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. RPT does not repair cartilage or ligaments, reverse arthritis, or ensure a particular return-to-sport date. Ask how your response and activity goals will be reviewed.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical care for inability to bear weight after injury, major deformity, or a hot, swollen knee with fever. A swollen painful calf also warrants urgent assessment. Seek assessment for a locking knee or symptoms that worsen or do not improve.
