## Why Muscles Feel Tight

Muscle tightness is a common description for a pulling, shortened, or restricted feeling. It can occur after a workout, a long commute, repetitive work, or a period of reduced activity. Sometimes it is local; sometimes it is part of a larger movement pattern involving nearby joints.

## Factors That May Contribute

Muscles respond to load, sleep, recovery, stress, and position. An old injury can lead to protective movement habits even after the original tissue has healed. Limited movement through the hips, upper back, or ankles can also shift demand to other areas.

## Gentle Targeted Care

RPT Clinic uses assessment findings to determine whether muscular tension is a relevant contributor. Robotic Precision Therapy can provide measured, gentle pressure to selected soft tissues as part of individualized care. Treatment focuses on comfortable movement and function, not forcing range of motion.

## When to Get Checked

Seek care for tightness with severe pain, progressive weakness, numbness, loss of coordination, fever, or symptoms following serious injury. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency muscle tightness.
