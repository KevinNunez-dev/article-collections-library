## Start With Movement Variety

Neck and shoulder tension often builds when the body stays in one position for too long. Short movement breaks, changing work position, and avoiding a constantly elevated shoulder can reduce demand on the upper trapezius and related muscles.

## Make Small Setup Adjustments

Place the screen near eye level, bring the keyboard and mouse close enough to avoid reaching, and support the arms when possible. For driving, adjust the seat so the shoulders can relax and take breaks on longer trips.

## Consider the Full Pattern

The neck, shoulder blades, chest, and upper back work together. RPT Clinic assesses this movement pattern and may use gentle targeted pressure for muscular tension when appropriate.

Seek urgent care for chest symptoms, sudden severe headache, fever, new arm weakness, loss of coordination, or symptoms after major trauma. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent neck and shoulder tension.
