## Is robotic massage the same as massage therapy?

Not necessarily. **Massage therapy uses hands-on techniques** that vary by practitioner and goal. At RPT Clinic, Robotic Precision Therapy (RPT) uses the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues under clinician direction after an assessment. People may use “robotic massage” for other devices or services, so ask what the particular provider means.

## What happens during each type of visit?

| Question | Massage therapy | RPT at RPT Clinic |
|---|---|---|
| How is pressure applied? | A massage therapist uses hands-on techniques; approaches vary | The RX2600 applies pressure to selected soft tissues under clinician direction |
| What happens first? | The practitioner may discuss goals, health history, and comfort | A clinician discusses symptoms and assesses whether treatment is appropriate |
| Can I speak up? | Ask to adjust pressure or stop | Ask the clinician to adjust pressure or stop |
| What should I compare? | Credentials, goals, techniques, price, and session terms | Assessment, treatment plan, price, and when progress is reviewed |

These are descriptions, not evidence that one approach is better, deeper, or longer lasting. Ask each provider what their specific service includes.

## What might an RPT appointment feel like?

The clinician selects appropriate areas for pressure after assessment. Sensations vary, and the pressure should be discussed throughout the visit. Share your medical history, medications, recent injuries, and any areas you do not want treated. Tell the clinician if you feel uncomfortable or want to stop. Ask what reactions to watch for afterward and whom to contact if symptoms change.

Massage practitioners also use different techniques and pressure levels. Discuss your preferences and health history before either service.

## How should you compare cost and payment?

Ask each provider for the first-visit price, follow-up price, what is included, and cancellation terms. RPT Clinic's [FAQ](https://rptclinic.com/faqs/) lists $225 for a 90-minute first appointment including assessment and treatment, and $150 for each subsequent one-hour treatment. The clinic is out-of-pocket and does not bill insurance. Confirm current prices, payment eligibility, and reimbursement with the clinic and your plan. See [RPT pricing and payment](/health/robotic-precision-therapy-cost-insurance-questions/).

## Which approach should you choose?

Start with your goals: comfort, relaxation, or help assessing a recurring problem may lead to different conversations. Ask what will be assessed, how progress will be reviewed, and when medical input is appropriate. RPT may address appropriate muscular contributors but is not a diagnosis or a replacement for necessary medical care. Read [massage vs. physical therapy for lower back pain](/health/massage-vs-physical-therapy-lower-back-pain/).

## When should you seek urgent medical care?

Seek emergency care for chest pain or trouble breathing, new loss of bladder or bowel control, saddle numbness, or rapidly worsening weakness. A painful swollen leg, severe pain after major trauma, or fever with severe pain needs urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for a massage or therapy appointment.
