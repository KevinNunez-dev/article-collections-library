## What is neck pain?

Neck pain, sometimes called cervical pain or cervicalgia, is discomfort in the area between the base of the skull and the upper shoulders. It can cause stiffness, soreness, headache, difficulty turning your head or pain that travels into the shoulder or arm.

## What can contribute to neck pain?

Common contributors include muscle tension, prolonged positioning, repetitive activity, injury, joint changes and nerve irritation. A clinician considers your symptoms, health history and physical examination when identifying next steps.

## Questions to consider before a visit

Keep track of when your symptoms began, whether they follow an injury, what movements affect them, whether pain travels into an arm and whether numbness or weakness is present.

## Treatment approaches

Depending on the assessment, care may include changes in activity, posture and ergonomics guidance, therapeutic exercise, rehabilitation and other treatments recommended by your healthcare team.

## When should you seek care quickly?

Seek urgent medical attention for neck pain with major injury, fever, a severe or unusual headache, confusion, new weakness, loss of coordination or other sudden neurological symptoms.
