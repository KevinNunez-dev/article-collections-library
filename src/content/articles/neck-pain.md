## What causes neck pain?

Neck pain, sometimes called cervicalgia, may feel like stiffness, soreness, or sharp pain. Muscle strain and joint irritation are possible contributors, as are injuries, nerve problems, and other medical conditions. Work habits or sleep positions may affect symptoms, but posture alone does not establish a diagnosis.

## Can neck pain travel into the arm or head?

Some people experience shoulder or arm pain, tingling, or headaches along with neck discomfort. These symptoms have different possible causes. Tell a clinician about new numbness, weakness, balance problems, or a changing headache rather than assuming muscle tension is responsible.

## How may Robotic Precision Therapy help?

Following an assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. RPT does not diagnose the cause, guarantee headache relief, or replace medical care for nerve symptoms. Tell the clinician if pressure is uncomfortable and ask how the plan will be reviewed.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical care for neck pain after major trauma, fever with a severe headache, new or worsening weakness, sudden loss of balance, confusion, or difficulty breathing or swallowing. A sudden severe or unusual headache needs emergency assessment. For persistent or changing symptoms, arrange a medical evaluation.
