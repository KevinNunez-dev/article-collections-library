## The short answer

**Cost and insurance for Robotic Precision Therapy depend on the clinic, your plan, and how services are billed.** Rates and terms change, so use this guide to ask the right questions and get current answers directly from the clinic and your insurer.

## Questions for the clinic

1. What does the first visit cost, and what does it include?
2. What do follow-up visits cost, and how long are they?
3. Are packages offered? What are the inclusion, expiration, refund, and cancellation terms?
4. Do you bill insurance, or do I pay and submit a claim myself?
5. What documentation do you provide for claims or HSA/FSA use?
6. What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

## Questions for your insurer

1. Is this provider and service covered under my plan?
2. How much of my deductible remains?
3. What are my copay or coinsurance amounts?
4. Is a referral or prior authorization required?
5. Is there a limit on visits per year?

## Red flags in cost conversations

- Pressure to commit to a large package before an assessment
- No clear answer on refunds or cancellations
- Promises of guaranteed results
- Unwillingness to put the estimate in writing

## When cost should not delay care

If you have severe pain after trauma, new weakness, numbness in the groin or saddle area, loss of bladder or bowel control, or a painful, swollen leg, seek urgent medical care first.
