## What is back pain?

Back pain is discomfort anywhere along the spine, often in the lower back. It may feel achy, stiff, sharp or burning, and it can affect movement, sleep, work and exercise. The right next step depends on the pattern of symptoms and your health history.

## Common back pain symptoms

- Stiffness or soreness in the low, middle or upper back.
- Pain that changes with sitting, standing, bending or lifting.
- Muscle tightness or reduced range of motion.
- Pain that travels into the buttock or leg.

## What can contribute to back pain?

Back symptoms can be linked to muscle or ligament strain, changes in activity, repetitive movement, joint or disc changes, posture, prior injury and other health conditions. An exam helps a clinician identify factors that may be relevant to you.

## How is back pain evaluated and treated?

A healthcare professional may ask when the pain began, what affects it, whether it travels, and whether you have numbness, weakness or other symptoms. Care can include education, activity guidance, therapeutic exercise, rehabilitation, medication discussions or other treatment options based on your assessment.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent evaluation for severe symptoms after major trauma, new or worsening weakness, numbness in the groin area, loss of bladder or bowel control, fever with back pain or other sudden concerning changes.
