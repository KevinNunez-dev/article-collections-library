## Back Pain Relief Through Robotic Precision Therapy

Back pain is one of the most common health complaints, affecting over 80% of people at some point in their lives. It ranges from a dull, constant ache to sudden, sharp pain and may radiate into the legs or other body parts. **Chronic back pain—pain lasting more than 12 weeks—rarely comes from a single event.** It develops from accumulated muscle imbalances, poor posture habits, and repetitive stress that go unaddressed over time.

At **RPT Clinic**, we treat the root cause: **muscular imbalance**. Using the **RX2600 Therapeutic Robot**, we deliver controlled, consistent pressure to specific muscles to release tension, restore alignment, and eliminate pain—without surgery or medication.

## What is Chronic Back Pain?

Chronic back pain involves discomfort that persists or recurs over 12+ weeks. It may feel:
- Achy, stiff, sharp, or burning
- Localized to the lower, middle, or upper back
- Radiating into the buttocks, legs, or hips
- Worse with sitting, standing, bending, or lifting

Unlike acute back pain from a single injury, chronic pain develops gradually as muscles become shortened, overworked, and imbalanced—often due to repetitive movements, poor posture, or previous injuries. This misalignment creates a cascade of problems: compressed nerves, increased pressure on joints and tendons, restricted range of motion, and inflammation.

## Common Root Causes of Chronic Back Pain

RPT Clinic evaluates both structural findings and functional movement patterns to identify what's actually driving your pain—not just where it hurts.

### Muscular Imbalances and Compensations
When muscles become shortened or overworked, they pull your spine out of proper alignment. Over time, this "misalignment syndrome" leads to stress on the lower back as your body compensates.

**Key signs of misalignment:**
- Uneven hips, shoulders, or gait
- Pain in the lower back, knees, or neck
- Stiffness or reduced range of motion
- Recurring injuries or pain shifting to different areas

### Prolonged Sitting and Sedentary Lifestyle
Sedentary habits weaken core stabilizers, allowing the spine to drift out of alignment. The psoas, erector spinae, and quadratus lumborum become shortened and tight.

### Herniated or Bulging Discs
Discs that protrude put pressure on nerve roots, especially at L4-L5 and L5-S1. While structural disc issues may require imaging, muscle imbalance often amplifies the pain through protective spasm.

### Degenerative Disc Disease (DDD)
Disc degeneration reduces height and shock absorption. However, **much of the pain comes not from the disc itself, but from surrounding muscles in protective spasm and nerve irritation caused by compression from misaligned vertebrae.**

### Sacroiliac Joint Dysfunction
Imbalanced muscles around the pelvis and hips can cause SI joint instability, leading to lower back and hip pain.

### Muscle Trigger Points
Hypersensitive knots in muscles refer pain to distant areas—particularly in the psoas, piriformis, and paraspinal muscles.

## When to Seek Urgent Care for Back Pain

Seek immediate medical evaluation for:
- Severe symptoms following major trauma or injury
- New or worsening weakness in the legs or feet
- Numbness in the groin area or loss of bowel/bladder control
- Fever combined with back pain
- Unexplained weight loss with back pain
- Other sudden, concerning changes

## Spinal Anatomy: Understanding Your Back

Your spine is supported by:
- **Vertebrae** — Bone segments stacked to form the spine's structure
- **Intervertebral Discs** — Cushions between vertebrae that absorb shock
- **Ligaments & Muscles** — Provide stability and support movement
- **Nerves** — Transmit signals from the brain and spinal cord

Proper alignment and function of each component are critical for pain-free movement. Muscle imbalances pull the spine out of alignment, creating stress on discs, joints, and nerves.
