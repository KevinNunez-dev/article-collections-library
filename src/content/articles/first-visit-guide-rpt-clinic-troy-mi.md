## Your visit in four steps

### 1. Book
Contact the clinic and ask about appointment length, cost, and what to bring. See [cost and insurance questions](/health/robotic-precision-therapy-cost-insurance-questions) for a list to work from.

### 2. Prepare
- Write down when symptoms began and what makes them better or worse
- List medications and past treatments
- Gather imaging reports or records
- Wear comfortable clothing

### 3. Arrive
The clinic is in Suite 426 on W Big Beaver Road. Check parking and entrance details with the clinic ahead of time.

### 4. Evaluate
A clinician reviews your history, observes movement, and assesses muscles, then discusses whether RPT appears appropriate. Read [how the evaluation works](/health/how-robotic-precision-therapy-evaluation-works) and the [first appointment guide](/health/first-robotic-precision-therapy-appointment) for detail.

## Go to urgent care instead if

You have severe pain after trauma, loss of bladder or bowel control, groin or saddle numbness, new weakness, chest pain, or a painful, swollen leg.
