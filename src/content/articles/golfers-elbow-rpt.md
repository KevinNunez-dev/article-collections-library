## Golfer's Elbow Relief: Return to Sport

Golfer's elbow (medial epicondylitis) causes pain on the inside of the elbow, especially with gripping, throwing, or repetitive arm movement. It affects golfers, baseball/softball players, tennis players, and anyone with repetitive flexor demand.

**The root cause: 90% of golfer's elbow involves tight forearm flexor muscles concentrating excessive force on the medial epicondyle where they attach.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we use the **RX2600 Therapeutic Robot** to release forearm flexor tension and restore arm balance. Most athletes notice improvement within 4-6 sessions and return to full athletic performance.

## Understanding Golfer's Elbow

Golfer's elbow develops from muscle imbalance and repetitive strain:
- Tight forearm flexors create excessive tension on attachment point
- Weak forearm muscles fail to distribute load properly
- Poor swing mechanics or throwing mechanics from tight muscles
- Repetitive gripping, throwing, or flexion increases strain
- Inadequate recovery allows inflammation to persist
- Muscle tightness in shoulder, neck, and chest compounds arm dysfunction

## Common Golfer's Elbow Symptoms

- Pain on inside of elbow
- Pain worse with gripping or squeezing
- Pain worse with throwing or swinging
- Pain worsening gradually (not sudden onset)
- Weak grip strength
- Swelling on medial elbow
- Morning stiffness or soreness
- Pain radiating down forearm toward wrist
- Difficulty throwing or swinging at full power

## The Root Challenge: Forearm Flexor Tightness + Weakness

Golfer's elbow results from specific forearm imbalance:

**Tight Forearm Flexors** (pronator teres, flexor carpi radialis, palmaris longus) create excessive force at medial epicondyle

**Weak Forearm Flexors** fail to distribute load during activity

**Tight Shoulder and Chest Muscles** alter arm mechanics increasing forearm strain

**Poor Swing/Throw Mechanics** from tight forearms increase elbow stress

**Repetitive Demands** without adequate recovery overwhelm already-imbalanced muscles

## Why Golfer's Elbow Treatment Plateaus

**Corticosteroid Injections**: Provide temporary relief but don't resolve muscle tension
