## Who Is a "Back Specialist" Near Me?

People searching for a **back specialist**, **spine specialist**, or **back doctor** near Troy, Michigan are usually asking one question: who should I see for my back pain? The answer depends on what your symptoms look like.

| Provider | Typically involved when |
| --- | --- |
| Primary care physician | First evaluation, medication questions, ruling out medical causes |
| Physical therapist | Back pain linked to movement, posture, muscle tension, or recurring strain |
| Physiatrist (rehabilitation physician) | Complex or persistent pain that needs medical management and a rehab plan |
| Spine or orthopedic surgeon | Findings that suggest a structural problem that may need a surgical opinion |
| Chiropractor | Spinal and joint-focused manual care, depending on the practitioner |

Most back pain is musculoskeletal and improves without surgery. See our overview of [back pain](/health/back-pain) for common causes.

## Red Flags: See a Physician or Emergency Care First

<div class="urgent-box">
<p class="cta-title">Get urgent medical care for back pain with:</p>
<ul>
<li>Loss of bladder or bowel control</li>
<li>Numbness in the groin, genitals, or inner thighs</li>
<li>Rapidly worsening leg weakness or foot drop</li>
<li>Fever, unexplained weight loss, or a history of cancer with new pain</li>
<li>Severe pain after a fall, accident, or trauma</li>
</ul>
</div>

## When a Spine Specialist or Surgeon May Make Sense

A surgical consultation is usually considered when symptoms, exam findings, and imaging line up and conservative care has not helped, or when there is progressive neurological loss. Many disc findings on imaging, including [bulging disc](/health/bulging-disc) and [herniated disc](/health/herniated-disc), are managed without surgery. For leg symptoms, see [sciatica treatment without surgery](/health/sciatica-treatment-without-surgery).

## Where Physical Therapy Fits

Physical therapy is often the first conservative step for non-emergency back pain. A therapist looks at spinal and hip mobility, strength, posture, and daily demands, then builds a plan. At RPT Clinic in Troy, Robotic Precision Therapy can apply controlled, repeatable pressure to selected soft tissues when assessment suggests a muscular contributor, combined with mobility and strengthening guidance. Individual results vary, and we refer you to a physician or spine surgeon when your findings call for it.

## What to Expect at Your First Visit

1. **History:** timeline, triggers, prior care, and imaging.
2. **Movement and strength assessment:** spine, hips, and nerve screening.
3. **Safety screening:** red-flag review and referral if needed.
4. **Plan:** whether RPT is appropriate and what care could look like.

## How to Choose a Provider

- They examine you instead of relying on imaging alone
- They explain likely contributors in plain language
- They screen for red flags and refer when needed
- They set clear goals and re-check progress
