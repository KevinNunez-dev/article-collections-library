## What counts as sciatica

Sciatica is pain, tingling, or burning that travels along the sciatic nerve path, usually from the buttock into the back of the leg. Disc, joint, and spinal causes are possible, and so are tight muscles around the hip and glutes. See [sciatic nerve pain in the leg](/health/sciatic-nerve-pain-in-leg/) and [piriformis syndrome vs sciatica](/health/piriformis-syndrome-vs-sciatica/).

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe unrelenting pain
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma
