## What Is a Muscle Spasm?

A muscle spasm is a sudden involuntary tightening that may feel like a knot, twitch, or cramp. It can occur in the calf, back, neck, foot, hand, or other areas. Many spasms are brief, but recurrent or severe symptoms deserve assessment.

## Common Triggers

Fatigue, a rapid increase in exercise, prolonged positions, muscle irritation after a strain, and inadequate recovery can contribute. Cramps can also be associated with medication effects or health conditions, so a complete history is important when they happen frequently.

## RPT Clinic Care

For non-emergency musculoskeletal patterns, RPT Clinic evaluates the involved area and nearby movement factors. Gentle, targeted pressure may be used when muscular guarding or tension is contributing to symptoms.

## When to Seek Care

Seek medical attention for spasms with new weakness, numbness, fever, severe swelling, dark urine, or symptoms that are frequent, severe, or worsening. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency muscular symptoms.
