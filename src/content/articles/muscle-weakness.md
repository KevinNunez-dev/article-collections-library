## Weakness Is Different From Feeling Tired

Muscle weakness means a reduced ability to produce expected force. It can make lifting an arm, climbing stairs, rising from a chair, gripping, or walking more difficult. This differs from normal fatigue after activity, though both can happen together.

## Why Assessment Matters

Pain, injury, reduced activity, nerve irritation, medication effects, and many medical conditions can contribute to weakness. New or progressive weakness should not be assumed to be caused by a tight muscle.

## When RPT Care May Be Relevant

After appropriate medical evaluation, RPT Clinic can assess whether muscular tension, pain-related guarding, or movement restriction contributes to a musculoskeletal limitation. Care is individualized and does not replace diagnosis or treatment of nerve or systemic conditions.

## Emergency Warning Signs

Call emergency services for sudden weakness on one side of the body, facial droop, difficulty speaking, new severe balance trouble, or sudden confusion. Seek timely medical evaluation for progressive weakness, repeated falls, or weakness with numbness.
