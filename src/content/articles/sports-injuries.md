## What are sports injuries?

Sports injuries affect muscles, tendons, ligaments, bones, joints and nerves. Some happen suddenly during a fall, collision or twist. Others develop gradually when training demands exceed recovery.

## Common sports injury types

- Sprains and strains.
- Tendon or muscle injuries.
- Overuse injuries such as shin pain.
- Joint injuries involving the knee, shoulder or ankle.
- Fractures and stress injuries.
- Concussions and other head injuries.

## Basic injury-prevention habits

- Progress training gradually.
- Build rest and recovery into your routine.
- Use appropriate equipment and footwear.
- Strengthen and condition for your sport.
- Address recurring pain before it becomes more limiting.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek prompt medical evaluation for deformity, inability to bear weight, severe swelling, a suspected fracture, head injury, loss of consciousness, numbness, weakness or severe pain after trauma.
