## Use Comfortable, Regular Movement

Hips may feel tight after sitting, driving, training, or a period of inactivity. Start with gentle, comfortable movement such as walking or controlled hip motion. Avoid forcing through sharp pain or trying to gain all range at once.

## Consider Nearby Areas

The hips work with the low back, pelvis, knees, ankles, and feet. A previous injury or limited movement elsewhere can change hip loading. An assessment can help identify relevant patterns.

## Assessment-Led Support

RPT Clinic evaluates movement and muscular tension around the hips and pelvis. When appropriate, gentle targeted pressure may be part of an individualized plan for comfortable movement.

Seek prompt care for sudden severe pain, a fall with inability to bear weight, fever with a hot joint, numbness, or progressive weakness. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency hip tightness.
