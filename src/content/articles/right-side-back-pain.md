## Why One Side of the Back Can Hurt

Right-side back pain may sit near the ribs, waist, or upper buttock. Some people notice it after twisting, carrying a bag on one shoulder, long periods at a desk, or a new activity. Muscles do not work in isolation: the back, ribs, hips, and abdomen all share load during walking, bending, and rotation.

## Mechanical Patterns That Can Matter

A strained muscle, protective tightening after an old injury, reduced hip motion, or a change in lifting mechanics can create one-sided discomfort. Tightness in the gluteal muscles or hip flexors may change how the pelvis moves; muscles along the spine may then compensate. An assessment can look at both the painful side and the movement pattern creating ongoing load.

## RPT Clinic's Assessment-Led Approach

When symptoms appear musculoskeletal, RPT Clinic may use Robotic Precision Therapy to provide measured, targeted pressure to areas of muscle tension. Treatment decisions are based on an in-person assessment and may include the lower back, hip, and adjacent soft tissues. The goal is to support movement quality and comfort, not to assume that all one-sided pain has a muscular cause.

## When Right-Side Pain Needs Medical Attention

Pain on the right side can also occur with conditions outside the musculoskeletal system. Seek prompt medical care for severe abdominal or flank pain, fever, vomiting, painful urination, blood in the urine, chest symptoms, a rash, or new weakness and numbness.

For persistent mechanical symptoms, [contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) to discuss an evaluation.
