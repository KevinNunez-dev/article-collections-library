## Who treats a herniated disc near Detroit?

A primary care clinician or rehabilitation physician can assess symptoms and help coordinate care. A spine specialist or surgeon may be involved when neurological findings, imaging, or persistent limitations call for a specialist opinion. A physical therapist may help with function when rehabilitation is appropriate.

The terms herniated disc and bulging disc describe changes in a spinal disc. They do not automatically explain every episode of back or leg pain. A clinician should connect the location on a scan, if one is available, with your symptoms and examination. Read about [herniated discs](/health/herniated-disc), [bulging discs](/health/bulging-disc), and the [AAOS guide to lumbar herniated discs](https://www.orthoinfo.org/bodypart/back/herniated-disk-in-the-lower-back/).

## What should the specialist evaluate?

Explain where pain starts and whether it travels into the leg or arm. Describe any numbness, tingling, weakness, changes in walking, and what makes symptoms better or worse. Bring the imaging report and prior treatment records if you have them; do not assume a new scan is needed before every visit.

The examination may include movement, strength, sensation, reflexes, and walking. Ask whether the imaging and examination fit together and whether further medical assessment is needed. A scan can show disc changes in people who do not have pain.

## When to seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical care for new loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the groin or saddle area, or rapidly worsening leg weakness. Severe back pain after major trauma also needs urgent assessment. New or worsening weakness in an arm or leg deserves prompt medical attention.

## Questions about treatment

- What findings make the disc a likely source of my symptoms?
- What options are reasonable now, and what are their limitations?
- How will strength, sensation, and daily function be monitored?
- When should I seek a spine specialist or surgical opinion?
- What should I do if symptoms worsen between visits?

Surgery is not automatic, but it can be important for selected situations. Discuss your options with an appropriate physician rather than relying on a guaranteed non-surgical claim.
