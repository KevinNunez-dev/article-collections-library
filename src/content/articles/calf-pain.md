## Calf Pain: Do Not Ignore the Pattern

The calf muscles power walking, climbing stairs, and push-off during running. A cramp, strain, or persistent tightness can limit activity, but new calf pain also deserves attention because not every cause is muscular.

## Movement-Related Contributors

Training changes, hills, tight footwear, previous ankle injury, and reduced ankle mobility may increase calf load. The gastrocnemius and soleus muscles connect to the heel through the Achilles tendon and affect both foot and knee movement.

## Gentle, Targeted Care

After an assessment rules out urgent concerns, RPT Clinic may use Robotic Precision Therapy to deliver controlled pressure to areas of muscular tension. The treatment plan can include mobility and activity guidance based on the person’s goals.

## Urgent Warning Signs

Get immediate medical help for one-sided calf swelling, redness, warmth, unexplained tenderness, chest pain, or shortness of breath. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency calf tightness or movement-related pain.
