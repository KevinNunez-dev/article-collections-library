## What is tennis elbow?

Tennis elbow is a common term for pain involving tendons on the outside of the elbow. It may develop with repetitive gripping, lifting, wrist extension or racquet-sport activity.

## Common symptoms

- Pain on the outside of the elbow.
- Pain with gripping, lifting or opening containers.
- Forearm aching or weakness.
- Symptoms that worsen with repetitive hand and wrist activity.

## What may help?

A clinician may recommend changes in activity, progressive exercise, rehabilitation, equipment or ergonomics review and other treatment options based on your symptoms.

## When should you seek care?

Seek evaluation for persistent symptoms, major weakness, numbness, swelling, pain after trauma or a loss of elbow movement.
