## Does RPT Clinic accept insurance?

**No. RPT Clinic is an out-of-pocket clinic and does not bill insurance.** The clinic lists cash and credit cards as payment options, along with HSA and FSA. CareCredit is identified for package treatments on the clinic's [package page](https://rptclinic.com/rpt-packages/).

Payment rules depend on what you are buying. Confirm current prices and accepted methods with the clinic before you book or purchase a package. For visit and package amounts, see [how much Robotic Precision Therapy costs](/health/robotic-precision-therapy-cost-insurance-questions).

## Can you submit an RPT Clinic invoice to your insurer?

Yes. RPT Clinic says it can provide an invoice with medical codes for you to submit to your insurer. Your plan determines whether it reimburses the service, what documentation it requires, and how much it may pay. An invoice is not a guarantee of reimbursement.

Before your appointment, call your insurer and ask:

- Does my plan reimburse this type of out-of-pocket service?
- Do I have out-of-network benefits, and what amount or limit applies?
- Do I need a referral, authorization, or specific documentation?
- What is the filing deadline, and how do I submit the invoice?
- Will you provide a written estimate, and does it guarantee claim payment?

Keep the insurer's response and reference number, but remember that a benefits estimate may differ from the final claim decision.

## Can you use HSA or FSA funds for RPT?

RPT Clinic lists Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) as payment options. Whether a particular treatment qualifies depends on the rules for your account and plan. Ask your account administrator what documentation is required and confirm eligibility before using the funds.

## Can you use CareCredit at RPT Clinic?

RPT Clinic's [package page](https://rptclinic.com/rpt-packages/) says CareCredit financing is available for package treatments. It does not clearly describe whether CareCredit applies to individual visits. Ask the clinic about the exact purchase and review the financing terms before you agree.

## What is the difference between insurance and CareCredit?

Insurance coverage is determined by your health plan. CareCredit is a financing option, not health insurance. Read the financing agreement and confirm the eligible purchase with the clinic; do not assume financing will be reimbursed by your insurer or HSA/FSA plan.

## What should you confirm before paying?

Ask for the current total, what the appointment or package includes, whether the initial assessment is included, and the cancellation, refund, transfer, or rescheduling terms. If you plan to submit an invoice or use HSA/FSA funds, confirm the documentation and eligibility rules before the appointment.
