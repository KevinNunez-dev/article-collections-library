## Lower Back Pain and Everyday Movement

Lower back pain affects the lumbar region above the hips. It can be sharp after a lift, stiff after sitting, or a recurring ache that makes walking, bending, or getting out of bed uncomfortable. The sensation alone does not identify the cause, which is why a symptom history and movement assessment matter.

## What Can Contribute to Low Back Symptoms?

The lumbar spine relies on coordinated support from the glutes, hip flexors, abdominal wall, hamstrings, and muscles alongside the spine. Repetitive bending, sudden lifting, long drives, deconditioning after injury, and rapid training changes can increase load on this system. Muscle guarding can then reduce normal motion and make routine activities feel more difficult.

## Targeted Care at RPT Clinic

RPT Clinic begins by identifying movement limits and areas of muscular sensitivity. Robotic Precision Therapy provides repeatable, gentle pressure to selected soft tissues as part of an individualized plan. Depending on the findings, treatment may address the low-back muscles as well as nearby hip and leg muscles that influence lumbar mechanics. Care is not a substitute for medical evaluation when symptoms suggest a nerve, fracture, inflammatory, or other non-muscular cause.

## Get Urgent Help for Red Flags

Seek urgent medical care for new bowel or bladder problems, numbness in the groin or saddle area, increasing leg weakness, fever with back pain, pain after major trauma, or unexplained weight loss. Arrange an evaluation for persistent pain, pain that radiates below the knee, or symptoms that limit daily life.
