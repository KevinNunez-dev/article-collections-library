## What are shin splints?

Shin splints is a common term for exercise-related pain along the lower leg, often the inner border of the shinbone. It frequently develops when training load changes faster than the body can adapt.

## Common signs of shin splints

- Tenderness along the inner lower leg.
- Pain during or after running, jumping or walking.
- Symptoms that begin after a change in mileage, intensity or surface.
- Discomfort that improves with rest but returns with activity.

## What can contribute?

Rapid training changes, running surfaces, footwear, muscle fatigue and movement mechanics can all be relevant. A clinician can assess pain patterns and help identify modifiable factors.

## What may help?

Care may include relative rest, a gradual return-to-activity plan, strength and mobility work, footwear review and rehabilitation based on your needs.

## When should you get checked?

Seek evaluation for focal pain, swelling, pain at rest, pain that worsens, inability to bear weight or symptoms that do not improve with activity changes.
