## What does lower back pain therapy cost?

There is no single price for physical therapy for lower back pain. Your cost depends on the provider, what the visit includes, how many appointments you choose, and any insurance benefits that apply. Ask for a written estimate before you decide, and compare the care plan as well as the price.

## Start with the first visit and follow-ups

Ask each provider for the evaluation price, what is included, the follow-up price and duration, and whether any services are billed separately. Ask when payment is due and whether there is a deposit or a cancellation fee. An estimate of visits is a planning tool, not a promise.

RPT Clinic's listed price is **$225 for a 90-minute first visit**, including assessment and treatment, and **$150 for each subsequent 60-minute session**. The clinic is out-of-pocket and does not bill insurance. These details were confirmed for this update on November 18, 2026; check current terms directly. The clinical team are DPTs trained in the RPT methodology.

For a simple example, one first visit plus three standard follow-ups is $225 + (3 × $150) = **$675 before any reimbursement**. This is arithmetic, not a recommended number of visits or an outcome prediction. Package terms, deposits, and other services may change the total. See the [RPT Clinic cost and insurance guide](/health/robotic-precision-therapy-cost-insurance-questions).

## Estimate insurance separately

For another provider, ask your insurer whether the exact clinician and service are in network, whether a deductible applies, and what copay, coinsurance, visit limits, or authorization requirements apply. An advertised charge is not necessarily the amount you owe.

RPT Clinic can provide an invoice with medical codes to submit to an insurer. Codes and an invoice do not establish coverage; reimbursement depends on the plan and is not guaranteed. Use the [lower back pain insurance question checklist](/health/lower-back-pain-therapy-insurance-questions) when you call. [HealthCare.gov's guide to total costs](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/your-total-costs/) explains the differences between premiums, deductibles, and other expenses.

## Add the practical costs

Estimate transportation and parking per visit. Separately, estimate appointment and travel time and any unpaid work you expect to miss. Do not count the same lost earnings twice. A nearby provider or longer appointment is not automatically a better clinical fit.

Ask what goals each clinician proposes, how progress will be checked, and what happens if the plan is not helping. See [how many physical therapy visits might be needed](/health/how-many-physical-therapy-visits-lower-back-pain) and the [sciatica copay-versus-cash worksheet](/health/sciatica-treatment-cost-copay-vs-cash).

## When not to wait

Seek urgent medical care for new or worsening leg weakness, numbness in the groin or saddle area, new loss of bladder or bowel control, or severe pain after major trauma. Fever with severe back pain also warrants prompt medical assessment.
