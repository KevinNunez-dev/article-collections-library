## Who treats foot and heel pain near Detroit?

A podiatrist specializes in foot and ankle care. Depending on the problem, a primary care clinician, sports medicine physician, orthopedic foot and ankle specialist, or physical therapist may also be involved. The right provider depends on whether the concern is an injury, skin or nail issue, nerve symptom, or movement-related pain.

Not every painful heel is plantar fasciitis. Pain under the heel, behind the heel, in the arch, or at the ball of the foot can have different explanations. See our [foot pain](/health/foot-pain) and [heel pain](/health/heel-pain) guides, as well as [MedlinePlus's heel pain overview](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003181.htm).

## What should the examination cover?

Point to the precise painful area and explain whether symptoms occur with the first steps, after standing, during exercise, or at rest. Mention a fall, change in activity, new footwear, swelling, numbness, a wound, or relevant conditions such as diabetes.

The clinician may examine the foot and ankle, walking, strength, and sensation. Bring the shoes you use most often if practical. Ask whether the examination suggests a need for imaging, podiatry, or another medical evaluation.

## When to seek medical care

Seek urgent medical assessment for a hot swollen foot with fever or inability to bear weight after an injury. If you have diabetes and a foot wound, seek prompt medical advice. Persistent numbness, a wound that is not healing, or pain that worsens also deserves medical assessment.

## Questions before choosing care

- What is the likely source, and what else could explain it?
- Should I see a podiatrist or have the foot medically evaluated first?
- What shoes or activity changes should I consider temporarily?
- How will we measure walking or standing tolerance?
- When should I return if symptoms persist?

If the pattern points toward plantar fasciitis, see the [plantar fasciitis guide](/health/plantar-fasciitis). A diagnosis and plan should fit your own examination rather than a search result alone.
