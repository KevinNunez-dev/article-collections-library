## Cerebral Palsy and Movement: Improve Your Mobility

Cerebral palsy (CP) is a lifelong condition affecting muscle tone, movement, and coordination. Severity ranges from mild to profound, with spasticity (muscle tightness) being a common challenge limiting function and independence.

At **RPT Clinic**, we provide personalized physical therapy programs addressing the specific movement challenges of cerebral palsy. Our approach combines therapy techniques with Robotic Precision Therapy to maximize functional outcomes.

## Understanding Cerebral Palsy

Cerebral palsy affects:
- Muscle tone (spasticity is most common)
- Movement control and coordination
- Balance and postural control
- Functional mobility and independence
- Often accompanied by pain from muscle tightness

## Common Movement Challenges

- Increased muscle tone (spasticity) limiting movement
- Difficulty with fine motor tasks
- Gait abnormalities
- Balance deficits
- Limited range of motion
- Pain from muscle tightness
- Functional dependence in daily activities

## Physical Therapy Goals for CP

**Improve Mobility**: Enhance range of motion and movement capability

**Reduce Spasticity**: Decrease muscle tightness and rigidity

**Improve Gait**: Enhance walking patterns and efficiency

**Improve Balance**: Reduce fall risk and increase confidence

**Enhance Independence**: Support daily living activities

**Reduce Pain**: Minimize discomfort from muscle tightness
