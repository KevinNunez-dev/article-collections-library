## Why Neck Pain Can Be Worse After Sleep

A stiff neck after sleep can happen when the neck stays in an uncomfortable position for hours, especially if a pillow is too high, too flat, or no longer supportive. Recent activity, stress-related muscle tension, and prolonged screen posture can make the neck more sensitive before bedtime.

## Practical Comfort Changes

Aim for a neutral, comfortable head and neck position rather than forcing the neck forward or to one side. Try changing pillow height or sleeping position gradually and avoid sleeping with the neck sharply rotated. During the day, use gentle, pain-free movement and brief posture changes instead of immobilizing the neck.

## When to Get Help

RPT Clinic evaluates muscular tension through the neck, shoulders, and upper back. For appropriate musculoskeletal patterns, gentle targeted pressure may be used within an individualized plan.

Seek urgent care for neck pain after major injury, fever with neck stiffness, new severe headache, arm weakness, trouble walking, or vision and speech changes. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency neck pain.
