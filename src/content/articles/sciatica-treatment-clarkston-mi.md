## What sciatica involves

Sciatica is pain, tingling, or burning that follows the sciatic nerve path, usually from the low back or buttock into the leg. Possible causes include disc problems, spinal narrowing, joint irritation, and tight muscles in the hip and glutes. See [sciatica from sitting](/health/sciatica-from-sitting/) for a common flare-up trigger, and [how to tell sciatica from piriformis pain](/health/how-to-tell-sciatica-from-piriformis-pain/).

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe unrelenting pain
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma
