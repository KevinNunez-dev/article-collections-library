## The short answer

**For most non-emergency back pain, the fastest relief comes from stacking simple steps: keep moving gently, ease tight muscles with heat or ice, take pressure off the spine, and stop repeating what triggered it.** If the pain lingers, assessment-led care at RPT Clinic in Troy can look at the muscles and movement patterns behind it.

## Why back pain is common in Troy

Troy has a large office and business corridor, and many people commute along I-75 and I-696. Long stretches in a chair or car are a frequent contributor to stiff hips, tight hamstrings, and a sore low back. Weekend yard work, snow shoveling, and gym sessions can add to that load.

## The top 10

### 1. Keep moving with low-impact activity

Rest briefly if needed, then walk. A lap around the office or a neighborhood block is usually kinder than bed rest. Stop if pain turns sharp or spreads.

### 2. Change position every 20 to 30 minutes

Stand during calls, walk to a printer, or take a short stop on a long drive. Our guide on [relieving back pain from sitting](/health/how-to-relieve-back-pain-from-sitting/) and the page on [poor posture](/health/poor-posture/) cover setup changes.

### 3. Use heat for stiff muscles

A heating pad or warm shower for up to 20 minutes can ease tension. Keep a layer between the heat and your skin.

### 4. Use ice for a fresh flare-up

After a sudden twist or lift, a wrapped cold pack for about 20 minutes may dull sharp pain. Pick whichever of heat or ice feels better.

### 5. Try a position of relief

Lie on your back with your calves on a chair, or on your side with a pillow between your knees. A rolled towel under the pelvis can also help the hips relax.

### 6. Do gentle, pain-free stretches

Try child's pose, cat-cow, a single knee to the chest, and light hamstring or glute stretches. Tight hips are a common partner of low back pain, so see [how to loosen tight hips](/health/how-to-loosen-tight-hips/).

### 7. Build core and hip support once it settles

Bridges, bird-dogs, and beginner yoga or Pilates can help the muscles that share the load with your back. Reduce the specific lifting or workout that provokes pain rather than stopping everything.

### 8. Use breathing and relaxation

Slow breathing and progressive muscle relaxation can help muscles that stay braced after a strain. [Muscle tightness](/health/muscle-tightness/) often eases more easily once the body feels safe to move.

### 9. Consider over-the-counter or topical options

Ask a pharmacist or doctor first. These can ease symptoms but do not explain why the back is guarding.

### 10. Get targeted care for the muscles driving the pain

If pain keeps coming back, read [why back pain keeps coming back](/health/why-does-my-back-pain-keep-coming-back/). Persistent pain is often linked to muscle tension and movement restrictions that an assessment can identify.

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe pain that is not relieved by rest
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma

See a clinician soon if pain is severe, travels down a leg, or has not improved in a couple of weeks. Read [when to see a doctor for back pain](/health/when-to-see-a-doctor-for-back-pain/).
