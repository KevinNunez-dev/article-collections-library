## What is a bulging disc?

Spinal discs are cushions between the bones of the spine. A bulging disc occurs when part of a disc extends beyond its usual edge. It may be found on imaging in people with or without pain.

## Possible symptoms

- Back or neck pain.
- Pain traveling into an arm or leg.
- Tingling, numbness or burning sensations.
- Muscle weakness or changes in movement.

## What can cause a bulging disc?

Disc changes can be associated with age-related changes, repetitive loading, injury and other factors. Symptoms depend on the spinal level involved and whether nearby nerves are affected.

## How is it evaluated?

A clinician reviews your symptoms, medical history, movement and neurological signs. Imaging may be recommended in certain situations, but imaging results are interpreted alongside the clinical examination.

## When is it urgent?

Seek urgent care for new or worsening weakness, numbness around the groin, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe symptoms after major trauma or other sudden neurological changes.
