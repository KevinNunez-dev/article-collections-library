## What Is a Bulging Disc?

Spinal discs are cushions between the vertebrae. A **bulging disc** means a disc has extended past its normal boundary. Disc bulges are common, increase with age, and often cause no symptoms. When one does cause symptoms, it is usually because it irritates a nearby nerve or the surrounding tissues.

## Bulging Disc Symptoms

- Low back or neck pain, often a deep ache
- Pain into the buttock, thigh, or leg (lumbar) or shoulder, arm, or hand (cervical)
- Numbness, tingling, or burning in a limb
- Muscle weakness in the leg, foot, or arm
- Back spasms and stiffness
- Symptoms that change with position

## Symptoms by Region

| Region | Common symptoms |
| --- | --- |
| Lower back | Back pain, pain into the buttock or leg, tingling, foot weakness |
| Mid-back | Band-like pain around the ribs, stiffness |
| Neck | Neck pain, pain into the shoulder or arm, hand tingling |

For nerve-type leg pain, see [sciatic nerve pain in the leg](/health/sciatic-nerve-pain-in-leg) and [sciatica](/health/sciatica).

## What Makes Symptoms Better or Worse

**Often worse with:** prolonged sitting, forward bending, lifting, coughing, sneezing, and driving.

**Often better with:** changing position, short walks, and gentle movement in a comfortable direction.

## Bulging Disc vs. Herniated Disc

A bulge extends outward without a tear through the outer layer; a herniation involves a tear with inner material pushing out. Imaging findings do not always match symptoms, so management is based on how you function. See [herniated disc](/health/herniated-disc).

## Patient Pathway

1. **Screen for red flags.** If any are present, seek urgent medical care.
2. **Stay active within comfort.** Avoid long bed rest.
3. **Modify triggers.** Break up sitting, avoid heavy bending and lifting for a short period.
4. **Book a physical therapy evaluation** if symptoms last beyond a couple of weeks or radiate.
5. **Follow a graded plan** of mobility, strength, and activity progression.
6. **Escalate** to a physician if symptoms progress, weakness develops, or pain remains disabling.

## How Physical Therapy Helps

A therapist identifies positions and movements that settle or provoke symptoms, then builds a plan of mobility, core and hip strength, and activity pacing.
