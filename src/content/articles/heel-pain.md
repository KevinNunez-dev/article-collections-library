## Heel Pain by Location

Heel pain can occur beneath the foot, at the back of the heel, or along the sides. Under-heel pain may be worse with first steps or prolonged standing. Back-of-heel pain may be more noticeable with shoes, stairs, and running.

## Common Contributors

A change in walking, standing, running, footwear, or work demands may increase heel load. Calf tightness, reduced ankle movement, and irritation of soft tissues around the heel can be relevant, but persistent symptoms need assessment.

## RPT Clinic Care

RPT Clinic evaluates calf, ankle, and foot movement to identify muscular contributors. For appropriate cases, Robotic Precision Therapy may use gentle, precise pressure to selected soft tissues within an individualized care plan.

## When to Seek Care

Seek prompt evaluation for severe pain after injury, inability to bear weight, marked redness or warmth, fever, numbness, or a wound on the foot. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency heel pain.
