## Pain on the Right Side of the Groin in Women

**Pain on the right side of the groin in women** can be muscular, joint-related, or linked to pelvic, urinary, gynecologic, or digestive causes. Location alone cannot separate them, so this guide covers musculoskeletal patterns and the signs that call for medical care. For the broader topic, see our [groin pain](/health/groin-pain) guide.

## Safety First

<div class="urgent-box">
<p class="cta-title">Seek prompt medical care for right groin or lower abdominal pain with:</p>
<ul>
<li>Fever, vomiting, or sudden severe pain</li>
<li>Pregnancy or a possibility of pregnancy</li>
<li>Unusual vaginal bleeding or discharge, or painful urination</li>
<li>A new groin bulge</li>
<li>Pain after a fall or trauma, or inability to bear weight</li>
</ul>
</div>

## Possible Musculoskeletal Contributors

- **Adductor or hip flexor strain:** pain with running, kicking, or stepping wide.
- **Hip joint irritation:** pain with deep flexion, rotation, or getting out of a car. See [hip pain](/health/hip-pain).
- **Abdominal wall tension:** pain with core activity, coughing, or twisting.
- **Pelvic floor muscle tension:** a deep pelvic or groin ache that may follow stress or prolonged sitting.
- **Low back and sacroiliac referral:** see [lower back pain](/health/lower-back-pain).
- **Glute and deep hip muscle tension:** see [glute pain](/health/glute-pain).

## Movement Clues

| Context | Pattern it may suggest |
| --- | --- |
| Walking or running | Adductor, hip joint, core control |
| Getting out of a car | Hip joint irritation |
| Squatting or lunging | Hip joint or adductor |
| Coughing or sneezing | Abdominal wall; new bulge needs medical exam |
| Prolonged sitting | Pelvic floor, hip flexor tension |

## How Physical Therapy Helps

After serious causes are addressed, a therapist reviews hip mobility, adductor and core strength, pelvic and back function, gait, and activity loading. RPT Clinic can apply gentle, targeted Robotic Precision Therapy pressure around the hip and thigh when a musculoskeletal pattern is identified, alongside strengthening and activity pacing. Care includes referral when symptoms suggest another cause.
