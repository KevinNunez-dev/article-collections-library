## How to Relieve Sciatica Pain Safely

Sciatica is a pattern of pain, tingling, numbness, or weakness that can travel from the low back or buttock into the leg. The best way to relieve it depends on what is irritating the nerve, how severe symptoms are, and whether there are warning signs.

## Practical First Steps

Avoid repeatedly forcing a painful bend, twist, or stretch. Change position regularly rather than staying seated or standing for long periods. Short, comfortable walks may be better tolerated than complete inactivity for some people. Notice whether symptoms travel farther down the leg, become weaker, or change with different positions; this information is useful in an assessment.

## Address the Movement Pattern

Low-back, hip, and gluteal muscles can become guarded when pain starts. RPT Clinic evaluates these movement patterns and, when appropriate, uses gentle targeted pressure to selected soft tissues as part of individualized care. This does not replace evaluation for disc, nerve, or other causes of sciatica.

## Know When to Seek Urgent Care

Seek urgent medical care for new bowel or bladder changes, numbness around the groin, progressive leg weakness, fever with back pain, or pain after major trauma. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency sciatica symptoms.
