## Why can ankle pain continue after a sprain?

An ankle sprain affects ligaments, but ongoing pain can also involve tendons, joints, muscles, or other injuries. A feeling that the ankle gives way should not automatically be attributed to one muscle. If pain persists or returns, a clinician can assess the ankle and advise whether further examination is needed.

## What should you discuss during recovery?

Describe how the injury happened, where the ankle hurts, whether it swells or gives way, and what you can comfortably do. Rehabilitation may include a gradual return to activity and exercises for movement, strength, and balance. The plan depends on the injury and its stage of recovery.

## Can Robotic Precision Therapy help ankle pain?

After assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. It does not repair a ligament, restore balance automatically, or guarantee prevention of future sprains. Ask how any soft-tissue care fits with rehabilitation and how progress will be reviewed.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical care for a deformed ankle, severe pain, or inability to bear weight after injury. A hot, swollen joint with fever or a painful swollen calf also needs urgent assessment. Seek medical care for persistent numbness or symptoms that worsen.
