## What is ankle pain?

Ankle pain can develop suddenly after a twist or fall, or gradually through repeated loading and activity. It may involve ligaments, tendons, muscles, joints, bones or nerves.

## Common ankle symptoms

- Pain with walking or putting weight on the foot.
- Swelling, bruising or stiffness.
- Instability or a feeling that the ankle may give out.
- Reduced range of motion or tenderness.

## What can cause ankle pain?

Sprains, strains, tendon concerns, overuse, arthritis, fractures and other conditions can cause similar symptoms. A clinician can assess the injury and recommend next steps.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent care for deformity, inability to bear weight, severe swelling, numbness, a cold or pale foot, uncontrolled bleeding or pain after a major injury.
