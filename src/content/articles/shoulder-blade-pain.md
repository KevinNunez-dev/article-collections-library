## Pain Around the Shoulder Blade

The shoulder blade, or scapula, is the connection point between the arm, upper back, ribs, and neck. Pain may feel like an ache under one shoulder blade, a tight band across the upper back, or discomfort with reaching, turning the head, and sitting at a desk.

## Why Scapular Muscles Become Overloaded

Repetitive overhead movement, prolonged forward-head posture, carrying loads, and a sudden increase in sport can change how the shoulder blade moves. Tight chest muscles, overworked upper trapezius muscles, and reduced motion through the upper back can make the supporting muscles work harder. Neck irritation may also refer discomfort toward the shoulder blade.

## RPT Clinic Care for Muscular Contributors

An RPT Clinic assessment reviews where symptoms begin, what movements provoke them, and how the shoulder, neck, and upper back move together. Robotic Precision Therapy may be used to apply precise, gentle pressure to selected areas of muscular tension. This is intended to complement movement-based care and does not replace evaluation of joint, nerve, or internal causes of pain.

## When to Get Checked Urgently

Seek urgent care for shoulder blade pain with chest pressure, difficulty breathing, sweating, nausea, fever, major trauma, new arm weakness, or loss of coordination. Arrange an evaluation for persistent pain, pain that wakes you at night, or numbness and tingling that travels into the hand.
