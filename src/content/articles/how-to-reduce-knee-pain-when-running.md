## Reduce the Load That Triggers Symptoms

A rapid increase in weekly mileage, hills, speed work, or running frequency can increase knee demand. Reduce the specific activity that provokes pain, and rebuild gradually once movement is comfortable.

## Review the Whole Lower Limb

The hips, thighs, calves, ankles, and feet all influence how force reaches the knee. Prior injury, shoes, surfaces, recovery, and fatigue can change the pattern. RPT Clinic assesses these factors and may use gentle targeted pressure for relevant muscular tension.

## Know When to Stop and Get Care

Seek prompt evaluation for swelling, locking, giving way, deformity, inability to bear weight, or pain after a significant twist or fall. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency running knee pain.
