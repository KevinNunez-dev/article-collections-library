## Inner Knee Pain and Movement

Inner knee pain, or medial knee pain, may feel sharp with a turn, sore after walking, or achy during stairs and squats. It can follow a specific incident or develop gradually as activity, strength, footwear, or movement patterns change.

## Potential Contributors

The inner knee can be stressed by a twist, changes in training volume, reduced hip control, limited ankle motion, or changes in how the foot contacts the ground. Muscles in the thigh, hip, and lower leg help control knee alignment during activity. When coordination or tolerance changes, the knee may be asked to handle load differently.

## RPT Clinic Care for Muscular Contributors

RPT Clinic uses an assessment to understand symptoms, prior injury, and movement pattern before recommending care. For appropriate musculoskeletal cases, Robotic Precision Therapy provides controlled, gentle pressure to selected muscle groups that may be contributing to stiffness or altered mechanics. The goal is to support more comfortable movement as part of an individualized plan.

## When to Get an Evaluation

Seek prompt medical care for substantial swelling, locking, instability, inability to bear weight, a hot or red joint with fever, or pain after a major impact. Ongoing pain that limits everyday tasks or does not improve with reasonable activity modification should also be assessed.
