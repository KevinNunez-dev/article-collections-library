## What can cause shoulder pain?

Shoulder pain may make reaching, lifting, or sleeping on one side uncomfortable. Possible causes include muscle strain, tendon injury, joint conditions, or pain referred from the neck or another area. A painful shoulder is not automatically a rotator cuff tear, and a symptom alone cannot identify its cause.

## What should you tell a clinician?

Describe when pain started, whether it followed an injury, which movements are difficult, and whether you have weakness, numbness, swelling, or night pain. A clinician can assess movement and discuss whether further examination or imaging is appropriate. Exercise-based rehabilitation, other therapy, medication, or surgery may be considered depending on the diagnosis and your goals.

## Where does Robotic Precision Therapy fit?

After assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. It does not repair a tendon or guarantee restored range of motion. Ask what the visit includes, how progress will be measured, and whether exercise or other care should be part of your plan.

See [neck pain](/health/neck-pain/) and [what RPT is and is not](/health/what-robotic-precision-therapy-is-and-is-not/).

## When should you seek urgent care?

Call emergency services for shoulder pain accompanied by chest pain, shortness of breath, or other possible heart attack symptoms. Seek urgent assessment after an injury with deformity, inability to move the arm, sudden weakness, or significant swelling. A hot, red joint with fever also needs urgent medical care.
