## What is shoulder pain?

Shoulder pain can occur in the shoulder joint itself or in nearby muscles, tendons, nerves and the neck. It may be felt while reaching, lifting, sleeping on one side or performing sports and work tasks.

## Symptoms to discuss with a clinician

Symptoms may include pain with movement, stiffness, weakness, reduced range of motion, clicking, swelling or pain that travels down the arm. Record when symptoms began and whether they followed a fall, repetitive activity or another injury.

## What can cause shoulder pain?

Possible contributors include overuse, strain, tendon concerns, joint changes, injury and referred pain from the neck. A qualified healthcare professional can evaluate your symptoms in context.

## Treatment options

Care may include activity modification, therapeutic exercise, rehabilitation, pain-management strategies, medication discussions or referral for additional evaluation. The appropriate approach depends on your diagnosis and goals.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical help for severe pain after injury, a visible deformity, sudden inability to move the arm, chest pain, difficulty breathing or new neurological symptoms.
