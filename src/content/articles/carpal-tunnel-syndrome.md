## What is carpal tunnel syndrome?

Carpal tunnel syndrome occurs when the median nerve is compressed as it passes through a narrow space in the wrist. It can affect sensation, comfort and hand strength.

## Common symptoms

Symptoms can include tingling, numbness, burning discomfort or weakness in the hand and fingers. Some people notice symptoms at night or while holding a phone, driving, typing or gripping objects.

## What can contribute to symptoms?

Repetitive hand and wrist activity, wrist position, swelling, injury and some health conditions can contribute. Similar symptoms can also come from other nerve or musculoskeletal conditions, making professional assessment important.

## Treatment approaches

A clinician may recommend activity or ergonomic changes, splinting, rehabilitation, medication discussions, testing or referral depending on your symptoms and examination.

## When should you seek care?

Make an appointment for persistent numbness, weakness, hand clumsiness, worsening symptoms or pain that interferes with work, sleep or daily activities.
