## What is carpal tunnel syndrome?

Carpal tunnel syndrome occurs when the median nerve is compressed as it passes through the wrist. Common symptoms include tingling or numbness in the thumb, index and middle fingers and part of the ring finger, often at night. Some people notice weakness or difficulty gripping objects.

## Is all hand numbness caused by carpal tunnel?

No. Other nerve or medical conditions can cause similar symptoms, and symptoms may occur in one or both hands. An appropriate medical assessment can help determine the cause and whether tests are needed. Do not assume that neck or shoulder tension is responsible for a compressed nerve at the wrist.

## What treatment options are available?

Depending on the situation, a clinician may discuss a wrist splint, changes to activities, medication, an injection, or surgery. Each has different indications and risks. Persistent numbness, weakness, or symptoms that interfere with daily activities deserve assessment rather than repeated care without review.

## What can Robotic Precision Therapy do?

After assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. RPT does not release the median nerve or guarantee that surgery can be avoided. Discuss it with the clinician managing your symptoms; do not postpone recommended medical care.

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical care for sudden weakness or numbness, especially with facial droop, speech changes, or other possible stroke symptoms; call emergency services if stroke is suspected. An injured, deformed hand or a hand that is hot, swollen, and accompanied by fever also needs urgent assessment.
