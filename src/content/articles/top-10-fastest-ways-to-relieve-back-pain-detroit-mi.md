## The short answer

**For non-emergency back pain, the fastest relief usually comes from stacking a few simple steps: stay gently active, calm tight muscles, take pressure off the spine, and stop repeating the trigger.** If pain stays, assessment-led care can look at what is keeping the muscles guarded.

## The top 10

### 1. Keep moving with low-impact activity

Short walks, easy swimming, or gentle yoga beat bed rest for most non-emergency back pain. Stop if pain turns sharp.

### 2. Break up commutes and desk time

Change position every 20 to 30 minutes when you can. Stop and stretch on longer drives, and see [how to relieve back pain from sitting](/health/how-to-relieve-back-pain-from-sitting/). Sciatica-like symptoms from sitting are covered in [sciatica from sitting](/health/sciatica-from-sitting/).

### 3. Use heat for stiff muscles

A heating pad or warm shower for up to 20 minutes can loosen tension. Protect your skin.

### 4. Use ice after a sudden strain

A wrapped cold pack for about 20 minutes may dull sharp pain after a twist or lift. Choose whichever of heat or ice feels better.

### 5. Try a position of relief

Calves on a chair while lying on your back, or a pillow between your knees on your side, can take load off the low back.

### 6. Do gentle stretches

Child's pose, cat-cow, knee to chest, and easy hamstring or glute stretches are good starting points. See [how to loosen tight hips](/health/how-to-loosen-tight-hips/) and [glute pain](/health/glute-pain/) for related areas.

### 7. Build core and hip support

Bridges, bird-dogs, and beginner yoga or Pilates can help share the load once the flare-up eases. Modify lifting rather than avoiding all activity.

### 8. Use breathing and relaxation

Slow breathing and progressive muscle relaxation can help muscles let go. Learn more about [muscle spasms](/health/muscle-spasms/) and [muscle stiffness](/health/muscle-stiffness/).

### 9. Consider over-the-counter or topical options

Check with a pharmacist or doctor first. They ease symptoms but do not address the reason the back keeps guarding.

### 10. Get targeted care for the muscles driving the pain

Massage can feel good, and physical therapy can build movement. See [massage vs physical therapy in Detroit](/health/massage-vs-physical-therapy-detroit/) and [massage for chronic back pain](/health/massage-for-chronic-back-pain-detroit/). When pain persists, an assessment can show whether muscular contributors are involved. This is where Robotic Precision Therapy fits.

## When to get urgent or prompt care

Seek urgent medical care for:

- Progressive weakness in a leg or foot
- Numbness in the groin or saddle area
- New bowel or bladder changes
- Fever or severe pain that rest does not relieve
- Pain after a fall, accident, or significant trauma

See a clinician soon if pain is severe, travels down a leg, or has not improved in a couple of weeks. Read [when to see a doctor for back pain](/health/when-to-see-a-doctor-for-back-pain/).
