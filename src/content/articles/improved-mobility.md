## What is mobility?

Mobility is the ability to move your body and joints with enough range, strength, coordination and control for daily activities. It can affect walking, reaching, dressing, exercise and independence.

## What can affect mobility?

- Pain, stiffness or swelling.
- Muscle weakness or reduced endurance.
- Balance or coordination changes.
- Injury, illness or surgery recovery.
- Fear of falling or reduced activity over time.

## Practical ways to support mobility

- Choose regular movement that fits your current ability.
- Build strength and balance gradually.
- Break up long periods of sitting.
- Use a gradual plan when returning after injury or illness.
- Ask for professional guidance when symptoms limit progress.

## When should you seek care?

Make an appointment for new or worsening weakness, unexplained falls, progressive loss of movement, severe pain or symptoms that interfere with daily tasks.
