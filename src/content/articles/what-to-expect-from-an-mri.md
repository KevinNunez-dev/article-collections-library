## What is an MRI?

Magnetic resonance imaging, commonly called MRI, is an imaging test that creates detailed pictures of areas inside the body. Your care team orders a scan based on your symptoms and clinical needs.

## How should you prepare?

Read your instructions carefully and ask questions before your appointment. Tell the imaging team about implanted devices, metal in your body, medicines, allergies and any chance you may be pregnant.

## What happens during the scan?

You may lie on a table that moves into the MRI machine. The equipment can be loud, and your team will tell you how to communicate during the exam.
