## What is a migraine?

Migraine is a neurological condition that can cause recurring episodes of head pain and other symptoms. Episodes vary in frequency and intensity, and they may affect work, sleep, family life and physical activity.

## Common migraine symptoms

- Moderate to severe head pain that may be one-sided or throbbing.
- Nausea or vomiting.
- Sensitivity to light, sound or smells.
- Changes in vision, sensation or speech for some people.

## Identifying potential triggers

A symptom diary can help you and your clinician look for patterns involving sleep, meals, hydration, stress, activity, hormones, medicines and environmental factors. Not every migraine has an obvious trigger.

## Treatment options

A healthcare professional can discuss treatment options based on your symptoms, migraine pattern, other health conditions and medicines. Treatment may include acute symptom management, prevention strategies and lifestyle guidance.

## Emergency warning signs

Call 911 or seek emergency care for a sudden severe headache, new headache with weakness or speech changes, fainting, seizure, fever and stiff neck, head injury or other alarming symptoms.
