## Achilles Tendon Pain and Activity

The Achilles tendon connects the calf muscles to the heel. Pain may be felt at the back of the heel or several inches above it, especially with stairs, running, jumping, or the first steps after rest.

## Why Symptoms Can Develop

Sudden increases in running, hills, speed work, or jumping can raise tendon demand. Tight or fatigued calf muscles, changes in ankle mobility, footwear, and recovery may also influence loading.

## Assessment-Led Care at RPT Clinic

RPT Clinic considers the calf, ankle, foot, and activity history when evaluating musculoskeletal contributors. When appropriate, Robotic Precision Therapy may use gentle, targeted pressure on selected muscular tissues as part of a plan that respects tendon tolerance and gradual loading.

## When to Seek Urgent Care

A sudden pop, a gap above the heel, rapid swelling, bruising, or inability to push off or stand on the toes requires prompt medical care. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for persistent non-emergency Achilles symptoms.
