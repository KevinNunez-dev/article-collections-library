## Who treats neck pain near Detroit?

A primary care clinician, rehabilitation physician, spine specialist, or physical therapist may evaluate neck pain. The best starting point depends on the pattern. Stiffness after a long workday needs a different conversation from pain after a collision or symptoms accompanied by worsening arm weakness.

A useful provider asks about your history and daily limitations rather than assuming every painful neck is caused by posture. See our [neck pain guide](/health/neck-pain) and [MedlinePlus's neck pain overview](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003025.htm).

## What should the appointment cover?

Tell the clinician where pain begins, whether it travels into the shoulder or arm, and whether you notice numbness, tingling, headaches, dizziness, or weakness. Explain what happens when driving, working at a screen, sleeping, or lifting.

The assessment may include neck and shoulder movement, arm strength and sensation, and a review of neurological or other warning signs. Mention recent injuries, prior surgery, relevant medical conditions, and previous treatment. Ask what the findings mean and when medical testing or referral is appropriate.

## When to seek medical care

Seek urgent medical care for severe neck pain after major trauma, or neck pain with fever and a severe headache. New or worsening arm weakness or problems with balance deserve prompt medical assessment. Sudden one-sided weakness with facial droop or speech difficulty is an emergency: call 911.

Pain that persists, interrupts sleep, or travels down an arm also warrants evaluation. Do not assume a massage or a stretch is the right answer before the symptom pattern is understood.

## Questions for a neck pain provider

- What findings point toward the neck, shoulder, or another source?
- Do my arm symptoms need a physician evaluation or further testing?
- Which activities can I continue, and what should I modify?
- How will we track progress and reassess if symptoms do not improve?
- What are the expected visit costs and payment arrangements?
