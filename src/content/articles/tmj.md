## What are TMJ disorders?

Temporomandibular joint disorders, often called TMJ or TMD, involve the jaw joints and surrounding muscles. Symptoms can affect chewing, speaking, yawning and everyday comfort.

## Common symptoms

- Jaw or facial pain.
- Clicking, popping or grating with jaw movement.
- Difficulty opening or closing the mouth fully.
- Headaches, ear-area discomfort or muscle tenderness.

## What can contribute to TMJ symptoms?

Jaw clenching, teeth grinding, stress, injury, bite changes and joint or muscle conditions may contribute. A clinician or dental professional can assess your symptoms and medical history.

## Treatment approaches

Care may include education, jaw-rest strategies, therapeutic exercise, stress-management support, dental evaluation or other individualized treatment. Avoid assuming a single treatment fits every cause.

## When should you seek prompt care?

Seek timely evaluation for sudden inability to open or close the jaw, significant swelling, fever, severe pain after injury or new neurological symptoms.
