## What Are Stiff Muscles?

**Stiff muscles** feel hard to move, tight, or slow to loosen up, often after rest, in the morning, or after unaccustomed exercise. Stiffness can involve muscles, joints, or both, and it is one of the most common musculoskeletal complaints.

## Differential Guide: What Pattern Do You Have?

| Pattern | Possible contributors |
| --- | --- |
| Stiff after sitting, eases with movement | Inactivity, posture, hip and back mobility |
| Stiff 1 to 2 days after new exercise | Delayed-onset muscle soreness |
| Stiff in one muscle with a pulling feeling | Muscle tightness, overuse |
| Stiff after an injury with pain on use | Strain; see [muscle strain](/health/muscle-strain) |
| Stiff with sudden knots or cramps | Spasm; see [muscle spasms](/health/muscle-spasms) |
| Morning stiffness lasting over an hour, joint swelling | Needs medical evaluation |
| Stiffness with weakness or numbness | Needs medical evaluation |

This table helps organize symptoms; it does not diagnose a condition.

## Common Causes

- **Inactivity and prolonged positions:** muscles and joints stiffen when they do not move.
- **Overuse and unfamiliar exercise:** muscle soreness and stiffness follow.
- **Posture and ergonomics:** sustained positions load the same muscles.
- **Cold and dehydration:** can make muscles feel stiffer.
- **Stress and poor sleep:** raise tension.
- **Limited joint mobility:** see [limited range of motion](/health/limited-range-of-motion).
- **Medical conditions:** inflammatory, thyroid, and neurological conditions can contribute.

## Morning and After-Rest Stiffness

Short-lived stiffness that eases within minutes of moving is common. Try gentle movement, a warm shower, and a few easy stretches. Stiffness that lasts an hour or longer, affects multiple joints, or comes with swelling should be evaluated.

## Stiffness vs. Tightness vs. Spasm

Tightness is ongoing tension in a muscle (see [muscle tightness](/health/muscle-tightness)), spasm is a sudden involuntary contraction, and stiffness is reduced ease of movement. They often occur together.

## Safe Ways to Relieve Stiffness

1. Move gently and often, and avoid long static positions.
2. Use warmth before activity.
3. Do slow, comfortable stretches.
4. Add gradual strengthening.
5. Stay hydrated and keep a regular sleep routine.
