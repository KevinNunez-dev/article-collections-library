## What Does Muscle Stiffness Feel Like?

Muscle stiffness is a feeling of tightness, resistance, or reduced ease of movement. It may occur in one area after exercise or be more widespread after a long day of sitting, repetitive work, stress, or an injury. Stiffness is a symptom, not a diagnosis, so the pattern matters.

## Common Contributors

Unfamiliar activity, rapid training changes, prolonged positioning, protective muscle guarding after injury, and reduced movement through a nearby joint can all contribute. Sleep, recovery, hydration, and work setup can also affect how muscles feel.

## RPT Clinic's Assessment-Led Approach

RPT Clinic considers the affected area and the movement chain around it. When appropriate, Robotic Precision Therapy provides controlled, gentle pressure to selected muscles that appear to contribute to tension or restricted motion. Care may be paired with gradual activity and mobility guidance.

## When to Seek Medical Care

Seek prompt medical care for stiffness with fever, a hot swollen joint, severe weakness, widespread pain after a new medication, dark urine, or symptoms after a major injury. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency stiffness that limits movement.
