## What supports sports performance?

Sports performance involves sport-specific skill, strength, conditioning, movement, recovery and overall health. The best plan is one that progresses gradually and fits your current training level.

## Five areas to review

- Training load: Increase volume and intensity in a planned way.
- Recovery: Make room for rest, sleep and lower-intensity sessions.
- Movement: Address mobility, strength and technique needs relevant to your sport.
- Fueling: Discuss nutrition and hydration needs with qualified professionals.
- Health: Pay attention to pain, fatigue and changes in performance.

## When should an athlete seek evaluation?

Consider an assessment for recurring pain, a new injury, persistent fatigue, a sudden performance decline, numbness, weakness or symptoms that change how you move.

## A practical next step

Track training, symptoms, sleep and recovery for two weeks. This record can help a coach or clinician identify patterns and build a more useful plan.
