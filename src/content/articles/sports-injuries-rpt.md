## Sports Injuries Relief: Get Back in the Game

Sports injuries range from acute tears to chronic overuse conditions. Whether from sudden trauma or repetitive strain, injuries trigger muscle guarding—a protective mechanism where muscles tighten around the injury to prevent movement.

**While muscle guarding protects initially, prolonged guarding limits blood flow, delays healing, and increases re-injury risk.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we use the **RX2600 Therapeutic Robot** to safely release muscle guarding while supporting the healing process. Most athletes notice significant improvement within 4-8 sessions and return to sport faster.

## Understanding Sports Injuries

Sports injuries include:
- Acute traumatic injuries (tears, sprains)
- Overuse injuries (tendinitis, muscle strains)
- Impact injuries (bruising, contusions)
- Repetitive strain injuries
- Combinations of the above

Injuries trigger:
- Immediate pain and inflammation
- Muscle guarding and spasm
- Limited range of motion
- Compensatory movement patterns
- Secondary muscle imbalances
- Reduced athletic performance

## Common Sports Injury Symptoms

- Immediate pain at injury site
- Swelling and inflammation
- Limited movement or stiffness
- Muscle weakness
- Instability or giving way
- Pain with specific movements
- Compensatory pain in other areas
- Difficulty returning to previous activity level

## The Root Challenge: Injury + Muscle Guarding + Secondary Imbalances

Sports injuries are complex because they trigger cascading problems:

**Initial Injury** - Tissue damage from trauma or overuse

**Immediate Muscle Guarding** - Muscles tighten protectively, limiting blood flow

**Prolonged Guarding** - Extended protection delays healing and causes stiffness

**Secondary Imbalances** - Compensation patterns create imbalances throughout kinetic chain

**Re-injury Risk** - Muscle imbalances predispose to re-injury

## Why Injuries Get Stuck

**Immobilization Helps Initially**: But prolonged immobilization causes muscle atrophy and stiffness

**Pain Creates Fear**: Fear of re-injury prevents normal movement patterns, cementing compensation patterns

**Rehabilitation May Not Address Muscle Guarding**: Standard PT doesn't always release the protective muscle tension

**Secondary Imbalances Go Unaddressed**: These set up re-injury scenarios
