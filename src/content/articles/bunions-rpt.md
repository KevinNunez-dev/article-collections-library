## Bunions Relief: Prevent Progression and Pain

Bunions (hallux valgus) are progressive deformities where the big toe angles toward the other toes, causing pain, swelling, and functional limitation. Bunions gradually worsen without intervention.

**The root cause: Foot muscle imbalance combined with genetic predisposition drives bunion development and progression.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we use the **RX2600 Therapeutic Robot** to release imbalanced foot muscles, preventing progression and reducing pain. Early intervention prevents surgical intervention.

## Understanding Bunions

Bunions develop from:
- Intrinsic foot muscle imbalance
- Genetic predisposition to foot structure
- Chronic foot pronation
- Tight calf and foot muscles
- Poor footwear choices
- Inadequate foot support

## Common Bunion Symptoms

- Pain and swelling at base of big toe
- Visible toe deviation
- Limited big toe movement
- Difficulty finding comfortable shoes
- Calluses or corns
- Progressive deformity
- Gait changes from altered foot mechanics
- Secondary foot pain from compensation

## The Root Challenge: Foot Muscle Imbalance

Bunions result from imbalanced foot muscles:

**Weak Intrinsic Foot Muscles** fail to support proper toe alignment

**Tight Calf Muscles** increase foot pronation

**Weak Posterior Tibialis** fails to support arch

**Foot Pronation** creates excessive force on big toe joint
