## What is plantar fasciitis?

Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of pain at the bottom of the heel. The plantar fascia is a band of tissue that supports the arch of the foot. Irritation in this area can make walking, standing and exercise uncomfortable.

## Common plantar fasciitis symptoms

- Sharp heel pain with first steps in the morning.
- Pain after sitting or resting for a long time.
- Soreness after standing, walking or running.
- Tenderness near the heel or along the arch.

## What can contribute to heel pain?

Several factors can contribute, including changes in activity, repetitive impact, prolonged standing, footwear, calf or foot tightness and individual foot mechanics. Heel pain can have other causes, so an evaluation may be useful when symptoms persist.

## What may be part of treatment?

A care plan may include activity guidance, footwear discussions, stretching or strengthening exercises, rehabilitation and other options based on your symptoms and medical history.

## When should you seek care?

Schedule an evaluation for pain that does not improve, trouble bearing weight, marked swelling, numbness, pain after injury or symptoms that limit everyday activities.
