## Why does your heel hurt when you take your first steps?

Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of pain near the heel, often noticeable with the first steps after sleeping or sitting. The plantar fascia is a band of tissue along the bottom of the foot. Other conditions can also cause heel pain, so the timing of pain alone is not a diagnosis.

## What should you tell a clinician?

Describe the location of pain, how long it has been present, any recent change in activity or footwear, and whether symptoms follow an injury. Mention numbness, swelling, or difficulty bearing weight. A clinician can assess your foot and discuss whether additional medical evaluation is needed.

## What treatment options can you discuss?

Depending on your situation, care may include adjusting activity, stretching or strengthening, footwear changes, or other treatments. Ask what to try, how long to monitor your response, and when to reconsider the plan. Do not assume that calf tightness is the sole cause or that any one approach prevents recurrence.

## Where might Robotic Precision Therapy fit?

After assessment, RPT Clinic may use the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues when muscular contributors appear appropriate to address. Results vary; RPT does not guarantee heel pain resolution or replace needed medical care. See [ankle pain](/health/ankle-pain/) and [what RPT is](/health/what-is-robotic-precision-therapy/).

## When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical assessment for severe pain or inability to bear weight after an injury, or a hot, swollen foot with fever. Seek medical advice for persistent numbness, worsening pain, or symptoms that do not improve.
