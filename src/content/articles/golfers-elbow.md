## What is golfer’s elbow?

Golfer’s elbow is a common name for pain involving tendons attached to the inner side of the elbow. It can develop from repeated gripping, lifting, throwing or wrist movement.

## Common symptoms

- Pain or tenderness on the inside of the elbow.
- Forearm discomfort with gripping or lifting.
- Stiffness or weakness in the hand or wrist.
- Symptoms during sports, tools, typing or daily tasks.

## What can contribute?

Training changes, repetitive work, poor technique, reduced conditioning and insufficient recovery can contribute. Other nerve or joint conditions can cause similar symptoms.

## Treatment approaches

Care may include activity modification, rehabilitation, progressive strengthening, technique review and other options based on evaluation findings.

## When should you seek care?

Make an appointment for persistent pain, weakness, numbness, swelling, pain after trauma or symptoms that limit work or sport.
