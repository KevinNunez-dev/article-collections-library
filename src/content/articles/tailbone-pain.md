## Tailbone Pain and Sitting Discomfort

Tailbone pain, also called coccyx pain, can make sitting, driving, or moving from sitting to standing uncomfortable. It may start after a backward fall or develop slowly after long periods of pressure in the area.

## What Can Contribute?

Direct injury, prolonged sitting, changes after pregnancy, and muscular tension around the pelvis and hips can contribute. Because symptoms can overlap with low-back, hip, pelvic, bowel, and skin conditions, persistent pain needs an appropriate assessment.

## RPT Clinic's Assessment-Led Care

For suitable musculoskeletal presentations, RPT Clinic evaluates movement through the low back, hips, and pelvis. Robotic Precision Therapy may use gentle, targeted pressure to selected external soft tissues that contribute to muscular tension. It is not a substitute for assessment of internal or traumatic causes.

## When to Seek Care

Seek prompt medical care for severe pain after a fall, fever, rectal bleeding, an unexplained lump, numbness, bowel or bladder changes, or pain that progressively worsens. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency tailbone pain.
