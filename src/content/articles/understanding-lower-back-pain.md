## What is lower back pain?

Lower back pain describes discomfort in the area between the bottom of your rib cage and your hips. It may feel dull, sharp, stiff or sore, and it can affect movement differently from person to person.

## What can contribute to it?

Many factors can affect back pain. These can include changes in activity, muscle strain, posture, previous injury and some health conditions. An evaluation can help clarify what may be contributing in your case.

## When is it urgent?

Seek urgent evaluation for sudden severe symptoms, significant weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, symptoms after a serious injury, or other concerning changes.
