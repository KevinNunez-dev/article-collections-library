## Pain Near the Sacroiliac Region

The sacroiliac joints connect the pelvis to the base of the spine. Pain in this region is often felt near the upper buttock or one side of the low back and may be aggravated by standing from a chair, stairs, long walks, or turning in bed.

## Movement and Muscle Factors

The pelvis transfers load between the trunk and legs. Hip mobility, gluteal muscle function, low-back movement, and a previous injury can influence how this region is loaded. Similar symptoms may also come from the hip, lumbar spine, or nerve irritation.

## RPT Clinic Assessment

RPT Clinic evaluates the low back, hips, and movement pattern before recommending care. In appropriate cases, Robotic Precision Therapy may provide gentle, controlled pressure to selected soft tissues that contribute to muscular guarding or restricted motion.

## When to Get Medical Care

Seek urgent care for fever, unexplained weight loss, new bowel or bladder changes, numbness in the saddle area, progressive weakness, or pain after major trauma. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency SI-region pain.
