## Foot Pain Can Occur in Many Areas

The foot has numerous joints, tendons, muscles, and nerves. Pain may be in the arch, heel, ball of the foot, toes, or outer edge. The details of onset, footwear, activity, and location guide a useful assessment.

## Common Patterns

Long standing shifts, a new walking or running routine, a change in shoes, a previous ankle injury, and limited ankle mobility can all alter foot loading. Some foot pain is local; other symptoms can be influenced by the calf and the rest of the lower limb.

## RPT Clinic's Approach to Muscular Factors

For appropriate musculoskeletal patterns, RPT Clinic evaluates movement through the foot, ankle, and lower leg. Robotic Precision Therapy may provide gentle targeted pressure to selected soft tissues as part of individualized care.

## When to Seek Medical Care

Seek prompt care for inability to bear weight, a deformity, severe swelling, a wound, increasing numbness, or a cold, pale, or blue foot. [Contact RPT Clinic](https://rptclinic.com/contact/) for non-emergency foot symptoms.
