## What is physical therapy?

Physical therapy is a healthcare service that helps people improve movement and function. A physical therapist evaluates how your body moves, listens to your goals and creates a care plan that fits your needs.

## What can physical therapy help with?

Physical therapy may be part of care for many concerns, including:

- Pain that affects everyday movement.
- Recovery after an injury, illness or procedure.
- Balance, strength or mobility changes.
- Preparation for a planned procedure or return to activity.

## What happens during treatment?

Your plan may include guided exercise, education about movement and activity, and other techniques your clinician recommends. Progress is reviewed over time, and the plan can change as your needs change.
