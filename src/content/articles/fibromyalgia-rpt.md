## Fibromyalgia Relief: Reclaim Your Vibrant Life

Fibromyalgia is a complex condition characterized by widespread musculoskeletal pain, chronic fatigue, sleep disruption, and heightened sensitivity to touch. Symptoms often feel mysterious because standard tests show no obvious structural damage—making fibromyalgia an "invisible" condition that others struggle to understand.

**But here's what research and our clinical experience show: While fibromyalgia involves nervous system sensitization, 90% of patients also have significant muscle imbalance that compounds pain and perpetuates the cycle.**

At **RPT Clinic**, we use the **RX2600 Therapeutic Robot** to gently release tight muscles and help retrain your nervous system's pain processing. Most fibromyalgia patients notice improvement within 8-12 sessions.

## Understanding Fibromyalgia

Fibromyalgia involves:
- Widespread musculoskeletal pain and tender points
- Heightened nervous system pain sensitivity (allodynia, hyperalgesia)
- Sleep disruption leading to increased pain sensitivity
- Chronic fatigue and reduced activity tolerance
- Often depression, anxiety, or mood disturbances

The condition affects your central nervous system's ability to process pain signals, amplifying normal sensations into severe discomfort.

## Common Fibromyalgia Symptoms

- Widespread body pain (neck, shoulders, chest, back, hips, knees)
- Tender points that hurt with light touch
- Deep muscle aching and fatigue
- Sleep problems and non-restorative sleep
- Morning stiffness lasting hours
- Difficulty concentrating ("fibro fog")
- Sensitivity to cold, heat, light, sound
- Mood changes and anxiety
- Reduced activity tolerance

## The Root Challenge: Muscle Imbalance + Nervous System Sensitization

Fibromyalgia is complex. It involves nervous system hypersensitivity plus muscle dysfunction:

**Muscle Imbalance** - Certain muscles remain chronically tight and overactive while others weaken, creating additional strain and compensation patterns that amplify pain

**Nervous System Sensitization** - Your pain pathways become overprotective, interpreting normal sensations as threatening pain

**Poor Sleep Quality** - Chronic pain disrupts sleep, and poor sleep increases pain sensitivity—creating a vicious cycle

**Movement Fear** - Fear of triggering pain causes reduced activity, leading to deconditioning and further muscle imbalance

## Why Traditional Fibromyalgia Treatment Plateaus

**Pain medications** mask symptoms but don't address muscle imbalance or nervous system retraining  
**Aggressive PT** can trigger flare-ups if not carefully calibrated  
**Massage** feels good temporarily but doesn't achieve sustained muscle lengthening  
**Standard manual therapy** causes inconsistent pressure, which can be triggering for sensitive nervous systems
