## What Is Physical Therapy: Your Complete Guide

Physical therapy (PT) is an evidence-based healthcare profession focused on improving movement, function, and quality of life. Physical therapists diagnose and treat movement dysfunction, pain, and limitation resulting from injury, surgery, disease, or aging.

At **RPT Clinic**, we provide comprehensive physical therapy addressing root causes of dysfunction, enabling true recovery and prevention of future problems.

## What Physical Therapists Do

Physical therapists:
- Evaluate movement and function
- Diagnose movement dysfunction
- Develop personalized treatment plans
- Provide manual therapy (hands-on treatment)
- Design therapeutic exercise programs
- Teach injury prevention strategies
- Guide rehabilitation after injury or surgery
- Support chronic disease management

## Conditions Physical Therapy Treats

**Orthopedic/Musculoskeletal**:
- Neck and back pain
- Joint pain and arthritis
- Muscle strains and sprains
- Ligament injuries
- Posture problems

**Neurological**:
- Stroke recovery
- Parkinson's disease
- Multiple sclerosis
- Spinal cord injury
- Cerebral palsy

**Sports Medicine**:
- Sports injury recovery
- Performance enhancement
- Return-to-sport training
- Injury prevention

**Post-Surgical**:
- Orthopedic surgery recovery
- Joint replacement rehabilitation
- ACL reconstruction
- Other surgical recovery

## Physical Therapy Treatment Components

**Evaluation** - Comprehensive assessment of movement, pain, strength, range of motion, and function

**Manual Therapy** - Hands-on techniques including massage, joint mobilization, and stretching

**Therapeutic Exercise** - Targeted exercises addressing specific dysfunctions

**Modalities** - Technologies like Robotic Precision Therapy supporting healing

**Patient Education** - Teaching proper movement, posture, and prevention strategies

**Home Exercise Programs** - Exercises performed between sessions for continued progress

## Physical Therapy Benefits

- Reduced pain and inflammation
- Improved strength and flexibility
- Enhanced movement and function
- Faster recovery from injury or surgery
- Prevention of re-injury
- Improved athletic performance
- Increased independence
- Better quality of life

## The Physical Therapy Process

### Initial Evaluation (60-90 minutes)
Comprehensive assessment of your condition, medical history, functional limitations, and treatment goals.

### Treatment Plan Development
Personalized plan addressing your specific needs with realistic goals and timeline.

### Regular Treatment Sessions (30-60 minutes)
Combination of manual therapy, exercise, and education progressing toward your goals.

### Progress Reassessment
Regular evaluation ensuring progress and adjusting plan as needed.

### Discharge Planning
Transition to home management with maintenance exercises preventing re-injury.

## Realistic Timeline for Recovery

**Acute Conditions**: 4-6 weeks typical

**Subacute Conditions**: 6-12 weeks typical

**Chronic Conditions**: 12+ weeks; requires long-term management

**Post-Surgical**: Varies by surgery; typically 8-12 weeks

**Individual variation is significant** depending on severity, compliance, and complicating factors

## When to See a Physical Therapist

- Recent injury causing pain or limitation
- Post-surgical recovery
- Chronic pain not improving
- Reduced mobility affecting function
- Desire to improve athletic performance
- Prevention of future problems
- Pain or limitation interfering with valued activities
