## What is cerebral palsy?

Cerebral palsy is a group of conditions that affects movement, muscle tone and coordination. It is caused by changes or injury to the developing brain and can affect people in different ways.

## Possible symptoms

- Differences in muscle tone, such as stiffness or floppiness.
- Challenges with balance, coordination or walking.
- Involuntary movements or tremors.
- Speech, swallowing, vision or learning differences for some people.

## Types of support

Support may include medical care, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, assistive technology, education planning and community resources. Plans should be tailored to the person's goals and needs.

## Questions to ask a care team

- Which daily activities are most difficult right now?
- What supports may improve comfort, safety or participation?
- Are there equipment, therapy or specialist resources to consider?
- How should the plan change as needs evolve?

## When should you contact a clinician?

Contact a qualified healthcare professional for new pain, loss of function, changes in swallowing or breathing, repeated falls, sudden neurological symptoms or concerns about daily safety.
