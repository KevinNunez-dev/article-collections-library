## When should joint pain be evaluated?

Consider making an appointment when pain does not improve, keeps returning or gets in the way of work, sleep, exercise or daily activities.

## Signs to discuss with a clinician

- Pain after a fall, accident or sports injury.
- Noticeable swelling, redness, warmth or a change in how the joint looks.
- Stiffness or reduced movement that persists.
- Pain with fever or feeling generally unwell.
- Numbness, weakness or trouble putting weight on the joint.
