## Who evaluates piriformis-type pain near Detroit?

Buttock pain that travels into the leg is sometimes called piriformis syndrome, but the label should not replace an examination. A primary care clinician, rehabilitation or sports medicine physician, or physical therapist may help evaluate the pattern and decide whether another specialist is needed.

The spine, hip, and other structures can produce overlapping symptoms. Sciatica describes a radiating symptom pattern; it does not by itself identify the cause. See [piriformis syndrome versus sciatica](/health/piriformis-syndrome-vs-sciatica) and [MedlinePlus's piriformis overview](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000776.htm).

## What should a careful assessment include?

Tell the clinician where the pain begins and how far it travels. Note whether sitting, driving, walking, stairs, or changing position affects it. Mention tingling, numbness, weakness, previous back or hip problems, and any injury.

Depending on the history, the examination may include hip and back movement, gait, strength, sensation, and tests that reproduce symptoms. Ask what findings support the working explanation and what other causes are being considered. There is no reason to force a painful stretch while waiting for an evaluation.

## When to seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical care for new loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the groin or saddle area, or rapidly worsening leg weakness. Severe symptoms after major trauma also need urgent assessment. New or worsening weakness or persistent neurological symptoms deserve prompt medical attention.

## Questions before starting care

- What makes this pattern more or less likely to involve the back, hip, or surrounding muscles?
- Do I need a physician evaluation or further testing?
- Which sitting, walking, or exercise changes are reasonable for now?
- How will we track function and decide whether the plan is helping?
- What symptoms mean I should stop and seek medical care?

An individualized plan might focus on tolerating a drive or returning to walking, rather than treating a label alone. Read [how to distinguish sciatica from piriformis pain](/health/how-to-tell-sciatica-from-piriformis-pain).
