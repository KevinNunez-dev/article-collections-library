## Understanding Lower Back Pain: The Complete Guide

Lower back pain is incredibly common, affecting 80% of adults at some point. It's often frightening and debilitating, but the good news: **most lower back pain results from treatable muscle imbalance and tension, not serious structural damage.**

Understanding your pain helps guide effective treatment. At **RPT Clinic**, we help patients understand their pain and recover through targeted therapy.

## Common Lower Back Pain Causes

**Muscle Strain** - Overstretched or micro-torn muscles from lifting, movement, or overuse

**Muscle Imbalance** - Tight muscles pulling on the spine, weak muscles failing to support

**Poor Posture** - Creates chronic muscle tension and spine stress

**Herniated Disc** - Disc material pressing on nerves

**Facet Joint Dysfunction** - Small spine joints becoming irritated

**Ligament Sprain** - Overstretched ligaments supporting the spine

**Muscle Spasm** - Protective muscle guarding limiting movement

**Combinations** - Most back pain involves multiple factors

## Back Pain Levels

**Acute Pain** (0-6 weeks) - Recent onset, often from specific incident

**Subacute Pain** (6-12 weeks) - Persisting, not yet chronic

**Chronic Pain** (12+ weeks) - Long-standing, requires specialized management

## Lower Back Pain Symptoms

- Dull, aching pain in lower back
- Sharp, stabbing pain in specific area
- Muscle tension and tightness
- Pain worse with certain movements
- Pain radiating to buttocks or legs
- Morning stiffness
- Reduced movement range
- Muscle weakness
- Pain limiting activities

## Understanding Your Pain

**Not All Back Pain Is Structural**: Many people have disc bulges or bone spurs without pain. Conversely, significant pain can occur with minimal structural findings.

**Your Pain Is Real**: Even when imaging shows nothing, your pain is genuine and treatable.

**Root Causes Matter**: Addressing root causes produces better outcomes than treating symptoms alone.

**Individual Variation**: Back pain is highly individual; treatment must be personalized.

## Effective Back Pain Treatment

**Address Root Causes**: Identify and treat actual pain sources

**Release Muscle Tension**: Reduce protective muscle guarding

**Improve Posture**: Eliminate chronic spine stress

**Strengthen Supporting Muscles**: Improve spinal stability

**Restore Movement**: Enable pain-free movement patterns

**Manage Inflammation**: Reduce tissue irritation

**Build Confidence**: Address fear-avoidance limiting recovery

## When to Seek Urgent Care

Seek immediate medical attention for:
- Severe traumatic injury
- Progressive neurological symptoms (numbness, weakness)
- Loss of bowel/bladder control
- Fever with back pain (possible infection)
- Unintentional weight loss with pain
- Osteoporosis with sudden severe pain

## Recovery Timeline

**Most acute back pain improves within 4-6 weeks** with appropriate treatment

**Subacute pain typically resolves within 8-12 weeks** with consistent therapy

**Chronic pain requires longer-term management** but significant improvement is achievable

**Results depend on compliance** with treatment and home exercises
