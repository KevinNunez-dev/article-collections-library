## What is Robotic Precision Therapy?

Robotic Precision Therapy (RPT) is the name RPT Clinic uses for an approach in which a clinician directs the RX2600 Therapeutic Robot to apply pressure to selected soft tissues after an assessment. It may address appropriate muscular contributors to discomfort. It is not a diagnosis, and results vary.

## Is RPT an injection or laser treatment?

**No.** RPT as described here uses physical pressure. An injection uses a needle to deliver a substance, while laser therapy uses light. These are different interventions with different purposes and risks. This distinction describes RPT itself, not a complete list of services offered by the clinic; ask the clinic about current services.

## Is RPT a brace?

No. A brace is an external support worn around part of the body; RPT is a treatment session using soft-tissue pressure. Do not stop wearing a prescribed brace without speaking with the professional who prescribed it.

## Is RPT robotic surgery?

No. The RX2600 is described as a soft-tissue pressure device, not a surgical robot. Surgery is an operation that may be recommended for particular conditions. RPT does not repair or reposition discs, release a compressed nerve, or guarantee that surgery can be avoided. If a specialist recommends surgery or further assessment, discuss your options and timing with that specialist.

## Is RPT the same as spinal decompression or massage?

Nonsurgical spinal decompression generally refers to traction, a pulling force. RPT applies pressure to selected soft tissues. Massage therapy uses hands-on techniques that vary by practitioner. None of these terms guarantees a particular outcome. Compare [spinal decompression vs. RPT](/health/spinal-decompression-vs-robotic-precision-therapy/) and [robotic massage vs. massage therapy](/health/robotic-massage-vs-massage-therapy/).

## What happens before and during an RPT visit?

You can discuss your symptoms, health history, goals, and prior care with the clinician. If treatment appears appropriate, the clinician selects areas for pressure and oversees the session. Tell them if pressure is uncomfortable and ask for it to be adjusted or stopped. Ask what to expect afterward and when the plan will be reviewed. See the [first appointment guide](/health/first-robotic-precision-therapy-appointment/).

## When is another kind of care needed first?

Seek emergency care for chest pain or difficulty breathing, new loss of bladder or bowel control, saddle numbness, or rapidly worsening weakness. Severe symptoms after major trauma or fever with severe pain also need urgent medical assessment. RPT is not a substitute for emergency care.
