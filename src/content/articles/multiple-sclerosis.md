## What is multiple sclerosis?

Multiple sclerosis, often called MS, is a condition that affects the central nervous system, which includes the brain and spinal cord. Symptoms and disease course vary widely from person to person.

## Possible MS symptoms

- Vision changes or eye pain.
- Numbness, tingling or weakness.
- Balance, coordination or walking changes.
- Fatigue and changes in concentration.
- Bladder or bowel symptoms.

## How is MS diagnosed?

Diagnosis requires evaluation by qualified medical professionals. A clinician may use a health history, neurological examination, imaging, laboratory testing and other information to evaluate symptoms.

## What can care include?

Care may involve neurology, disease-modifying therapies, symptom management, rehabilitation, mental-health support and practical planning for daily function.

## When is urgent medical care needed?

Seek prompt medical care for sudden weakness, new loss of vision, severe headache, confusion, trouble speaking, chest pain, difficulty breathing or other sudden concerning symptoms.
