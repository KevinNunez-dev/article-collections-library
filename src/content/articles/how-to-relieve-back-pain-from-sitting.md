## Break Up Long Sitting Periods

Sitting can become uncomfortable when the back, hips, and legs stay in one position for hours. Instead of aiming for a single perfect posture, change position regularly. Stand, walk briefly, or shift tasks at intervals that feel manageable.

## Adjust Your Work Area

Keep frequently used items close, place the screen at a comfortable height, and support the lower body so you are not constantly reaching or twisting. Driving setup matters too: adjust the seat to avoid a long reach to the pedals or wheel.

## Get Help for Persistent Symptoms

RPT Clinic evaluates non-emergency back symptoms with attention to hips, legs, and movement habits. Gentle targeted pressure may be used for muscular contributors when appropriate. Seek urgent care for progressive weakness, numbness in the saddle area, fever, or bowel or bladder changes.
